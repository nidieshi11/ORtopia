\section*{独立存储 MILP}
保留的核心模型是多周期存储调度MILP模型，该模型具有互斥的充电和发电模式，以及充电状态动态变化。以终端能量值为目标的利润目标，是最通用且可以直接求解的版本。

\begin{equation}
\max \quad C^\top g - D^\top p
\tag{M1}
\end{equation}

受…的约束
\begin{equation}
\underline{p}\, u_t \le p_t \le \overline{p}\, u_t,
\qquad \forall t=1,\dots,T,
\tag{M2}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\underline{g}\, v_t \le g_t \le \overline{g}\, v_t,
\qquad \forall t=1,\dots,T,
\tag{M3}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
u_t + v_t \le 1,
\qquad \forall t=1,\dots,T,
\tag{M4}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
s_t = s_{t-1} + \alpha p_t - \beta g_t,
\qquad \forall t=1,\dots,T,
\tag{M5}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\underline{s} \le s_0 \le \overline{s},
\tag{M6}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
s_{t-1} + \alpha p_t \le \overline{s},
\qquad \forall t=1,\dots,T,
\tag{M7}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
s_{t-1} - \beta g_t \ge \underline{s},
\qquad \forall t=1,\dots,T,
\tag{M8}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
p_t \ge 0,\quad g_t \ge 0,\quad s_t \in \mathbb{R},
\qquad \forall t=1,\dots,T,
\tag{M9}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
u_t \in \{0,1\},\quad v_t \in \{0,1\},
\qquad \forall t=1,\dots,T.
\tag{M10}
\end{equation}

\noindent
如果需要固定的终端充电状态，则可以额外施加…
\begin{equation}
s_0 + \sum_{\tau=1}^T (\alpha p_\tau - \beta g_\tau) = s_T^{\mathrm{target}}.
\tag{M11}
\end{equation}
