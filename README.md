# ORtopia

按领域浏览 206 道运筹优化问题，或在论文集中按期刊查找原文。打开题目后，场景图独占一行。点击图片即可放大查看细节。图下方是数学模型和问题描述，可以切换有符号版与纯文字版。

P0455–P0499 的场景图采用题库 2026 年 11 月 22 日修订的版本。[查看题库修图记录](https://github.com/nidieshi11/ORtopia-bench/commit/bc5f5bb66a1eeec001035159c59822fa1d8f825b)

## 实例仿真

25 个实例覆盖全部 12 个领域，提供逐步回放和方案对照。点击题目标题下方的「进入实例仿真」，或从顶部导航的「实例仿真」浏览全部实例。在 P0213 调节天然气交付量和入口压力，观察沿线压力表和用户站能否达标，在 P0214 安排检修车和设备停机，在 P0310 改变物资的装载与去向，在 P0057 为不同电价时段分配燃料，在 P0062 操作抽水和发电。P0013 可以扳动炉前开关，沿选定的一条风电情景路径播放三期调度。P0045 可以改动客户的渠道份额，检查总收益、预算和公平约束。

| 领域 | 可操作的实例 |
| --- | --- |
| 城市道路交通 | P0068 · 调整共享单车调运路线 |
| 城市轨道交通 | P0154 · 调节通勤地铁发车间隔 |
| 民航、铁路与海运港口 | P0204 · 分配集装箱泊位与岸桥 |

## 本地浏览

在 GitHub 仓库中选择 **Code → Download ZIP**，解压整个文件夹，再用浏览器打开根目录的 `index.html`。场景图、公式渲染组件和论文 PDF 均随网页保存，可以离线浏览。

## Browse in English

ORtopia contains 206 operations research problems. Browse by domain or use the paper library to find articles by journal. Each problem opens with a full-width scenario diagram. Select it to zoom in. Below it are the mathematical model and the problem description, with a choice of symbolic or plain-text wording.

The scene diagrams for P0455–P0499 use the November 22, 2026 revision. [View the source revision](https://github.com/nidieshi11/ORtopia-bench/commit/bc5f5bb66a1eeec001035159c59822fa1d8f825b)

Twenty-five instances span all 12 domains and include step-by-step replays and alternatives. Open **进入实例仿真** beneath the problem title, or select **实例仿真** in the top navigation to browse the full collection. Adjust inlet pressure and delivery in P0213 to keep the gas network within its operating limits. Schedule maintenance crews in P0214, redirect relief shipments in P0310, allocate fuel across price periods in P0057, or switch between pumping and generation in P0062. In P0013, switch the thermal unit on or off and follow one wind scenario through three periods. In P0045 you can reallocate marketing shares and check total returns, budgets, and fairness constraints.

The bike rebalancing (P0068), metro scheduling (P0154), berth and crane assignment (P0204), graph partitioning (P0198), and cloud capacity (P0058) scenes each show how a change in a decision affects the example instance.

To view the site offline, select **Code → Download ZIP**, extract the entire folder, and open `index.html` in a browser. The archive includes the papers, diagrams, and math rendering assets.
