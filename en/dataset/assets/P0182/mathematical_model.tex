\section*{Fixed-speed RouteOptimization VRPTW}
This is the routing subproblem on PDF page15, equations(59)--(63),
including its explicit references to(17)--(19),(21) on page8.

Let $N$ be customers, $o$ the start depot, $e$ its distinct return copy,
$V=N\cup\{o,e\}$, and $A\subset V\times V$ the supplied arcs with no
incoming arcs to$o$, outgoing arcs from$e$, or self-arcs.
Given demands$q_i>0$, capacity$Q>0$, fleet limit$K\ge1$,
arc energy$\widehat E_{ij}$, distance$d_{ij}>0$, speed$\widehat v_{ij}>0$,
time constants$T_{ij}$, and windows$[\underline\tau_i,\overline\tau_i]$:
\begin{align*}
\min\quad&\sum_{(i,j)\in A}\widehat E_{ij}x_{ij}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad&
\left\lceil\frac{\sum_{i\in N}q_i}{Q}\right\rceil
\le\sum_{(o,j)\in A}x_{oj}\le K,\\
&\sum_{(i,j)\in A}x_{ij}=1 &&i\in N,\\
&\sum_{(i,j)\in A}x_{ij}-\sum_{(j,i)\in A}x_{ji}=0 &&i\in N,\\
&t_i+w_i+d_{ij}/\widehat v_{ij}-t_j\le(1-x_{ij})T_{ij}&&(i,j)\in A,\\
&\sum_{(i,j)\in\delta^+(\Omega)}x_{ij}\ge
\left\lceil\frac{\sum_{i\in\Omega}q_i}{Q}\right\rceil
&&\varnothing\ne\Omega\subseteq N,\\
&\underline\tau_i\le t_i\le\overline\tau_i&&i\in V,\\
&x_{ij}\in\{0,1\}&&(i,j)\in A,\\
&w_i\ge0&&i\in N\cup\{o\}.
\end{align*}
The waiting/recharging offset cannot be negative. In these fixed-speed routing
instances the cut sets range over customers and exclude both depots. All demands, fleet and depot mappings are
explicit input data. The construction uses realistic heterogeneous travel,
demands and time windows, and retains source coordinates where available.
