Basis and scope of the revision

According to pages 6 and 8–9 of the source document, the summation and integer domain issues need to be resolved. The new requirement for plugs is defined as max(0, total required plugs – existing plugs), allowing for the retention of existing facilities that are not in use. All original budget branches, candidate stations, limited capabilities, and objectives remain unchanged.

Problem: It involves integer linear programming problems. The paper transforms a complex nonlinear optimization problem from previous studies (Opt-Fast-Char) into an efficient integer linear programming model, under the reasonable assumption that the number of feasible charging points is limited—for example, only those located at endpoints of routes. This problem is a comprehensive network design issue, incorporating aspects such as fleet composition and allocation, facility location (charging stations, substations), resource coordination (charging equipment and electricity supply), as well as scenario-based charging scheme selection. The core innovation of the model lies in introducing the concept of “feasible charging scenarios” to linearize the charging logic.

Domain and physical completion

nbrbc,nvrb,ncjc,npjc,ncjrc(ct),ncjrc(b),ncjrcAll values are non-negative integers and satisfy the finite upper bound mentioned above (integer description on page 9 of the source text). The utptin Equation 4 represents a single power supply parameter, and no omitted products are allowed. The example provides exclusive limited power supply options for each station, as well as all existing stations/old vehicles/old plugs. Old facilities can be left unused without being deleted. The maximum non-negative value for new plugs is defined by the repair process; negative ”new construction quantities” are prohibited to offset investment costs. Each budget is independently and completely solved; the constant operational terms in Equation 3 are retained according to the selected model, without arbitrarily adding decision variables.
