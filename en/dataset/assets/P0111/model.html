Core model: bounded dispatch-menu assortment with driver decline

Source: Sections 2 and 3.1, PDF pp.4–6, utility (1), choice (2)–(3), objective (4)–(5), limits (6)–(7), and independent-choice probability (12). One complete general application model is retained. Instance parameters a,b fix the menu-exposure policy.

Inputs and meaning. D and O are the finite sets of idle drivers and unmatched orders in one batching window. a ∈{1,…,|D|} is the maximum number of menus containing an order; b ∈{1,…,|O|} is maximum menu size. Larger supplied bounds are equivalent to these finite maxima. Input udo ≥ 0 is the attractiveness of order o to driver d, and udc > 0 is the decline attractiveness. They may be supplied directly or evaluated using the paper’s estimated behavioral coefficients:

udo = β0d + β1dIo + β2dfo − β3dτ(ld,loorg) + β 4dV (lodest). (1)

Here Io is order type, fo fare, ld driver location, loorg,lodest the order endpoints, τ pickup time, and V future spatial destination value. The proportional probability rule uses fixed nonnegative calibrated utilities.

Single binary decision domain. xdo ∈{0,1} means that order o appears in driver d’s menu. The subsequent acceptance and assignment events follow the probability expressions below. Define deterministic expressions

Zd(x) = udc + ∑ o∈Oudoxdo > 0, pdo(x) = udoxdo Zd(x) ,pdc(x) = udc Zd(x), Po(x) = 1 −∏ d∈D(1 − pdo(x)).

Each driver selects at most one displayed order or declines, independently of other drivers. Thus Po counts an order selected by at least one driver exactly once, even when several select it. A tie is resolved in favor of the nearest selecting driver as in Section 2. An unlisted order has pdo = 0, and an empty menu has decline probability one, defining the empty-set case.

The objective is expected distinct successful matches. Capacity here concerns the displayed menu size. Behavioral normalization and collision probability remain part of this single model; homogeneous-case analyses, independent disjoint objective, unlimited joint-menu specialization and bundled-order extension are omitted.
