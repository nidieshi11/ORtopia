Sets, fixed data, and variable roles

The directed network is G(N,A). The set N consists of nodes, A of directed road arcs, I ⊆N of potential warehouse sites, T of relief-item types, and L of disaster levels. The problem-description paragraph assumes that candidate locations and demand nodes are the same; Table 1 nevertheless states I ⊆N, and the displayed model uses I as retained above.

The fixed nonnegative parameters fi,Si,st,cta,ctp,ctu,cth,ci,jt are, respectively, facility-opening cost, warehouse storage capacity, unit storage requirement, unit acquisition cost, unit post-disaster procurement cost, unit unmet-demand penalty, unit holding cost, and unit transportation cost. Their indices are i ∈N, t ∈T, and (i,j) ∈A, as applicable. The parameter vt is arc capacity required per unit of item t; V ^i,j is nominal arc capacity. The source assumes no limit on supplies available for first-stage prepositioning. It describes post-disaster procurement as limited and more expensive, without specifying a general numerical cost ratio.

The first-stage decision oi indicates whether a facility is open, and zt,i is its prepositioned quantity of item t. The five families αt,i,l,ϕt,i,l,γi,l,λi,l,τi,j,l are moment-dual variables, not physical inventory or flow variables. Appendix B specifies them as unrestricted in sign, for t ∈T, i ∈ I, l ∈ L, and (i,j) ∈A as appropriate. The scalar δ is the full recourse epigraph variable; no nonnegativity restriction on δ is printed.
