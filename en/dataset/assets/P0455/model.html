Fixed inputs, information, and economic definitions

The procurement time τ is fixed in this formulation. The producer is the Stackelberg leader; the retailer responds to the offered price menu. Negotiation occurs at t = 0, procurement and production at τ, and retail clearance at T. The common input restrictions are

T > 0,0 ≤ τ ≤ T,A ≥ 0,ξ > 0.

Here A is the random market-size parameter, ξ is a known constant, B is the retailer’s fixed finite initial budget, and cτ is the constant per-unit production cost at the fixed procurement time. The paper assumes that cτ is increasing in τ and that production capacity is unlimited. All model parameters are common knowledge. Both agents are risk neutral and value payoffs using the same already selected equivalent martingale measure ℚ.

On the probability space (Ω, ℱ , ℚ), the filtration {ℱ t}0≤t≤T is generated by the tradeable security-price process Xt. The random variable A is ℱ -measurable but not ℱ T-measurable; in particular, ℱ T≠ℱ . Financial returns and A are dependent. The only information available about A before procurement is that learned from Xt over [0,τ]. Conditional expectation and the demand forecast are defined by

Financial admissibility and the endogenous budget

There is a riskless cash account whose interest rate is identically zero. Thus Xt is a ℚ-martingale. Selecting the paper’s continuous-time version, the gain of an ℱ t-predictable self-financing strategy is

Gτ(𝜃) := ∫ 0τ𝜃 sdXs, 		(3)

where 𝜃s is the number of security units held at time s; self-financing determines the cash-account position. Subject to the paper’s unspecified integrability restrictions on feasible strategies, Gt(𝜃) is a ℚ-martingale.

The complete-financial-markets assumption states that every suitably integrable ℱ τ-measurable contingent claim is attainable by an ℱ t-predictable self-financing strategy. The paper therefore optimizes over claims and drops the argument 𝜃. Its stated restriction on the gain process is

Printed monotonicity inconsistency. The printed wording is recorded here but is not an additional model condition.
