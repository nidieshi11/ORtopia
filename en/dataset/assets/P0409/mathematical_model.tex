\section*{P409: rolling-stock allocation and a discrete timetable}
This is the fully specified time-expanded MILP executed by the current
\texttt{05\_solver\_repaired.py}. Source: paper Table 1 and model (5), pp.7--8.
The missing nonnegative integer allocation variable is restored. This is a
completed two-terminal network instance with the constraints of model (5).

\paragraph{Network and data.}
$D=\{d_1,d_2\}$, $L=\{l_1,l_2\}$, and
$\mathcal T=\{0,\ldots,H\}$ are depots, lines and one-minute time points.
The completed instances have $H=480$ and service start horizon $H_s=420$.
For each line use station states $A^\uparrow,B^\uparrow,
B^\downarrow,A^\downarrow$.
At each integer time create one-minute waiting arcs at these states,
service arcs $A^\uparrow\to B^\uparrow$ and
$B^\downarrow\to A^\downarrow$ of duration $v_l$ starting before $H_s$,
and turnaround arcs $B^\uparrow\to B^\downarrow$ and
$A^\downarrow\to A^\uparrow$ of duration $\tau$.
Pull-out arcs $d_1\to A^\uparrow,d_2\to B^\downarrow$ start before
$H_s$; pull-in arcs $A^\downarrow\to d_1,B^\uparrow\to d_2$
are allowed until $H$. Their duration is $\eta_l$.
Keep only arcs ending no later than $H$.
Every arc has one binary capacity. These rules define $A$ completely;
the six physical template arcs expand into time-indexed arcs by these rules.
Both depots serve both lines in the completed inputs.

$c_d$ is depot capacity, $Z_{\max}=\sum_dc_d$,
$\lambda_d=50000$, and $h_l=\lceil h_l^{\rm sec}/60\rceil$.
Period $k$ is the half-open interval $[b_k,e_k)$ in supplied order.
$A^i_{lk}$ is the set of direction-$i$ service arcs starting in that
period, and $\alpha^i_{lk}$ is the supplied service-count target
(the same input count is used in both directions).
Cost weights $\omega_1,\omega_2$ are explicit instance data.
The raw-cost and normalized-cost inputs are two different objective
parameterizations and are reported separately.

\paragraph{Variables and objective.}
\[
x_a\in\{0,1\},\quad
z_d\in\{0,\ldots,c_d\},\quad
r_{dl}\in\mathbb Z_+,\quad \phi^i_{lk}\ge0.
\]
\[
\min\quad
\omega_1\sum_{d\in D}\lambda_d z_d+
\omega_2\sum_{l,k}\sum_{i=0}^1\phi^i_{lk}.
\]
Let $\delta^\pm(v)$ be the incoming/outgoing arcs at a non-depot
time-expanded station node, with $+$ denoting outgoing.
Let $P^+_{dl}(t)$ be pull-out arcs of depot $d$, line $l$ starting
at time at most $t$, and $P^-_{dl}(t)$ the corresponding pull-ins
ending at time at most $t$. Let $E^{i,+}_{l}(t)$ and
$E^{i,-}_{l}(t)$ be direction-$i$ service starts and ends in
$[t,\min(H+1,t+h_l))$.
The complete feasible domain is
\begin{align}
&\sum_d z_d\le Z_{\max},&
&\sum_l r_{dl}\le z_d &&(d\in D),\\
&\sum_{a\in P^+_{dl}(t)}x_a-\sum_{a\in P^-_{dl}(t)}x_a
 \le r_{dl} &&&&(d,l,\ t<H),\\
&\sum_{a\in P^+_{dl}(H)}x_a-\sum_{a\in P^-_{dl}(H)}x_a=0
 &&&&(d,l),\\
&\sum_{a\in\delta^+(v)}x_a-\sum_{a\in\delta^-(v)}x_a=0
 &&&&(\text{all non-depot nodes }v),\\
&\sum_{a\in E^{i,+}_l(t)}x_a\le1,\qquad
 \sum_{a\in E^{i,-}_l(t)}x_a\le1 &&&&(l,i,t),\\
&\phi^i_{lk}\ge\alpha^i_{lk}-\sum_{a\in A^i_{lk}}x_a,\qquad
 \phi^i_{lk}\ge\sum_{a\in A^i_{lk}}x_a-\alpha^i_{lk}
 &&&&(l,i,k).
\end{align}
Empty arc sums are zero. Positive arc durations and station conservation
make the depot inventory inequality a physical availability constraint.
Integer-minute separation $h_l$ enforces the supplied second-based minimum
headway exactly on this grid. The optimum is defined on this discrete time grid.
All coefficients, graph rules and constraints above correspond to code
lines 6--50; result checks at lines 53--75 independently reconstruct
station balances, depot inventories, headways, fleet and objective.
