Enter and access the replica.

W represents heterogeneous fleets, V represents customers who require guaranteed service, while D represents routes that involve detours to charging stations. Separate start and end locations are used in o,e; sufficient copies of the charging stations and storage facilities are provided for multiple visits. These should be distinguished from the destination charging stations, which require a maximum of one visit per customer. The set of copies is denoted as F. A represents all self-looping arcs allowed on {o}∪ V ∪ F ∪{e}. These arcs neither enter o nor leave e. The fleet can use the empty route (o,e), which has zero time/distance/energy consumption. The number of copies must cover all possible visits within the specified time range. If there is a limit on the number of copies, then this model only considers instances within that limit.

The mapping from a time to its travel period is H(t). The travel time, estimated energy consumption, and distance in period h are given by τhij,ehij,dhij, respectively. τis,qi gives service time and customer demand, both zero at non-customer nodes. For vehicle v, the departure time T̲v, depot return deadline T¯v, post-return charging deadline T¯cv, battery bounds B̲v,B¯v, initial and terminal battery levels Biniv,Bterv, payload limit Q¯v, and charging power Pcv are given. The charging power at a node is Pni, with effective power piv = min ⁡ (Pcv,Pni). Charging time is undefined at nodes without charging, where Civ = Eiv = 0 is enforced. τf is the charging setup time. There is no queueing, and all times use a common time coordinate.
