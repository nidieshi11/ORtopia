\section*{P0348 Scheduling Electric Bus Trips and Full Recharges at Time-Varying Prices}
\subsection*{Mathematical formulation}
\[
\min\ c_f\sum_{j\in V}x_{oj}
 +\sum_{i\in V,j\in R:(i,j)\in A}
  x_{ij}\int_{\tau^a_{ij}}^{\tau^b_{ij}}E(t)\,dt.\tag{1}
\]

\begin{align}
\sum_{j:(i,j)\in A}x_{ij}&=1 &&i\in V,\tag{2}\\
\sum_{j:(i,j)\in A}x_{ij}&\le1 &&i\in R,\tag{3}\\
\sum_{i:(i,j)\in A}x_{ij}&=\sum_{k:(j,k)\in A}x_{jk}
&&j\in V\cup R,\tag{4}\\
\sum_{j\in V}x_{oj}&=\sum_{i\in V}x_{ie}\le N_{\rm EB}.\tag{12}
\end{align}

\begin{align}
x_{ij}=1&\Longrightarrow b_i+t_{ij}\le a_j &&i,j\in V,\tag{5}\\
x_{ij}=1&\Longrightarrow \tau^a_{ij}=b_i+t_{ij} &&i\in V,\ j\in R,\tag{6}\\
x_{ij}=1&\Longrightarrow \tau^b_{ij}=\tau^a_{ij}+(Q-q_j)/g
&&i\in V,\ j\in R,\tag{7}\\
x_{ij}=x_{jk}=1&\Longrightarrow \tau^b_{ij}+t_{jk}\le a_k
&&i,k\in V,\ j\in R,\tag{8}\\
x_{ij}=1&\Longrightarrow q_j\le q_i-u_i-d_{ij} &&i,j\in V,\tag{9a}\\
q_j&\ge u_j+h_j &&j\in V,\tag{9b}\\
x_{ij}=1&\Longrightarrow 0\le q_j\le q_i-u_i-d_{ij}
&&i\in V,\ j\in R,\tag{10}\\
x_{oj}=1&\Longrightarrow 0\le q_j\le Q-d_{oj} &&j\in V,\tag{11a}\\
x_{ij}=1&\Longrightarrow 0\le q_j\le Q-d_{ij} &&i\in R,\ j\in V,\tag{11b}\\
x_{ie}=1&\Longrightarrow q_i-u_i-d_{ie}\ge0 &&i\in V,\tag{11c}\\
x_{ij}&\in\{0,1\},\quad 0\le q_i\le Q,\quad
0\le\tau^a_{ij}\le\tau^b_{ij}.\tag{13}
\end{align}

\subsection*{Definitions and model scope}
A bus operator must cover given scheduled trips $V$, each with fixed start time $a_i$, end time $b_i$ and trip energy use $u_i$. There is one physical charging station, represented by enough distinct charging-visit copies $R$ to permit one recharge after every trip, and separate departure depot $o$ and return depot $e$ copies of the same depot. The supplied allowed connections $A$ go from departure depot to trips, between trips, from trips to charging copies, from charging copies to trips and from trips to return depot. No self-connection or connection between charging copies is allowed. Each connection has given travel time $t_{ij}$ and travel energy use $d_{ij}$.

Choose zero-or-one connection selections $x_{ij}$. Every trip has exactly one selected outgoing connection, every charging copy has at most one, and every trip or charging copy has equal selected incoming and outgoing counts. The number of selected connections leaving the departure depot equals the number entering the return depot and cannot exceed the given electric-bus allowance $N_{\rm EB}$. All trips must belong to connected chains from departure depot to return depot, including when degenerate zero-duration inputs would otherwise permit a closed chain disconnected from the depot.

Choose a continuous arrival energy $q_i$ at each trip or charging copy between zero and the given battery capacity $Q$. At every trip, arrival energy must be at least that trip's energy use plus its given post-trip safety energy $h_i$. On a selected connection between trips, or from a trip to a charging copy, the destination arrival energy cannot exceed the origin arrival energy less origin trip energy use and connection travel energy use. On a selected depot-to-trip connection, arrival energy cannot exceed battery capacity less connection energy use. On a selected charging-to-trip connection, it cannot exceed that same full-battery remainder. A selected trip-to-return-depot connection requires origin arrival energy less trip use and return travel use to be nonnegative. These are upper and lower energy requirements, not equalities forcing each reported arrival energy to its physical upper limit.

A selected direct trip-to-trip connection requires the first trip's fixed end plus travel time to be no later than the next trip's fixed start. For each trip-to-charging connection, choose nonnegative charging start $\tau^a_{ij}$ and charging end $\tau^b_{ij}$ with start no later than end. Both are zero if the connection is unselected. If selected, charging starts exactly at the first trip's end plus travel time and ends after battery capacity less arrival energy at that charging copy, divided by the strictly positive charging rate $g$. When both that charging connection and its successor connection to another trip are selected, charging end plus successor travel time cannot exceed the successor trip's fixed start.

The operator seeks the smallest daily vehicle charge plus actual charging expense. Charge the given daily vehicle fee $c_f$ for each selected depot departure. For each selected trip-to-charging connection, integrate the given time-varying charging cost rate $E(t)$ from charging start to charging end, then add across these connections. The rate is currency per unit time: if the input is a per-energy price, first multiply it by charging rate to obtain the cost rate. A recharge spanning several price intervals pays each interval's actual rate; an unselected charging event costs zero. Waiting to deliberately delay charging after station arrival and partial charging are not added as alternatives.
