Collections, parameters, and variables

V Refers to bus schedules with a fixed start time ai, end time bi, and task power consumption ui. R Represents copies of visits to a single charging station. o,e Represents copies of departures and returns from the same parking lot. A Includes the allowed o → V , V → V , V → R, R → V , and V → e arcs; does not include self-arcs or arcs between charging stations. The number of copies must be sufficient for the next charge for each schedule. tij,dij Represents the travel time and power consumption of an arc. The battery capacity is Q,the daily fixed cost of the vehicle is cf,the charging rate is g > 0(power/country),and the maximum number of vehicles is NEB. hi Represents the original safety limit for energy consumption from the end of schedule i until at the charging station or parking lot. E(t) Is the charging cost rate in the integral formula (1). If the input is the unit price of power p(t),E(t) = gp(t) should be provided first to make the integral unit monetary. Table 2 refers to it as electricity price, but formula (1) directly integrates by time. The costs are calculated based on the electricity price for each period and the above unit conversion.

xij ∈{0,1} represents a connecting arc; qi ∈ [0,Q] represents the battery level at the arrival at the shift/charging station; τija,τijb ≥ 0 is the start/end time of the charging copy j after the shift i. The cost of unselected charging arcs is zero, as are their two-time points.

Equation (11c) clearly indicates the energy required for returning to the station. If hi already covers the power consumption associated with station operations, then it can be considered redundant. Only connections between shifts from o to e are allowed. Fixed trips are connected in strict time order, which rules out cycles when travel or charging time is positive. For inputs with zero duration, standard connectivity constraints must be applied. Cycles disconnected from the depot are forbidden.

Original form and explanatory notes Equation (5) originally had the subscript marked as j ∈ R. However, this equation and its terms represent two consecutive periods, so it has been changed to j ∈ V . Equation (8) becomes effective when both the charging arc and subsequent arcs are selected. For equations (9) to (10), the Q option for those arcs that are not selected still remains active. This option is still denoted as ui. Here, the terms “consecutive periods/charging arcs” are used to clarify the conditions, thereby avoiding incorrect constraints on energy levels due to the selection of certain arcs. The energy status is determined using the upper bound relationships provided in the original formula. In the old LaTeX format, the reverse integration and multiplication operations were denoted as g. These have been corrected in the PDF version to represent integration from start to end, along with division by g.
