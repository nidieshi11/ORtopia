Core model: two-stage aircraft recovery with precancellation

Source: Section 3.1, PDF pp.4–5, Table 3 and (1)–(9). The complete route-based BM is retained; the nonanticipative first-stage precancellation and all scenario recovery decisions form one model.

Data and feasible routes. L,A,S,B are flights, aircraft, disruption scenarios and required end bases. ps > 0, ∑ ⁡ sps = 1. Costs cl and dls > cl are advance and real-time cancellation costs. cb ≥ 0 penalizes base imbalance and nb is the required number at base b. Os indexes airport/interval slot restrictions with integer capacity uos. For aircraft a and scenario s, Ras is the complete set of physically feasible routes on its finite time-space network, with every route satisfying the network requirements below. Each route starts at the aircraft’s actual available airport/time, uses compatible flight copies (scheduled time plus an allowed nonnegative delay), and connects successive flights only when airports agree and the previous arrival plus required turn time does not exceed the next departure. It respects aircraft availability/disruption restrictions, terminates within the recovery horizon, and contains at most one copy of each flight. Ground waiting arcs connect consecutive events at the same airport. An idle route can represent an aircraft staying at its starting base. These rules define Ras from the supplied flight times, delay-copy grid, aircraft availability, compatibility and turn-time data.

For each route, δlr indicates inclusion of flight l, 𝜃or counts its departures/arrivals in slot o, and γbr indicates its end base. Its cost is

qar = ∑ l∈Lδlr(kal + φlr),

where kal is aircraft swapping cost and φlr the delay cost of the selected copy. These are route coefficients.

Variables. xl ∈{0,1} is advance cancellation and is common to every scenario; yls ∈{0,1} is real-time cancellation; zars ∈{0,1} selects a route; vbs ∈ ℤ+ is the nonnegative base-shortage slack. The source prints ℤ in (9), but defines v as a penalized slack for missing aircraft. This document makes that physical nonnegative domain explicit.
