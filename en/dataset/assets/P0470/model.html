Common operational assumptions and printed qualifications

The problem description on physical pages 4–5 requires each customer to be served exactly once by either vehicle, with truck-only customers served by trucks. Each deployed homogeneous pair starts at and returns to the single depot. A drone may serve multiple customers in a flight, subject to payload and battery capacity, and may be launched and retrieved repeatedly along its truck’s route, at different locations. At each customer location, a drone can be launched and retrieved at most once. When the paired vehicles reconvene, the one arriving first waits for the other. Customer nodes have drone aprons, so drone waiting energy is negligible. A retrieved drone receives a fully charged replacement battery. Vehicles start service immediately upon arrival at customers; trucks may wait after service before departing, including at the depot. Soft-window violations incur earliness or lateness penalties, and a return after lmax incurs a late-return penalty rather than a hard return-time restriction.
