Basis and scope of the revision

According to the definitions on pages 4–7 of the source text, where each task is performed once per vehicle, and all tasks are carried out together, the condition i=j should be excluded. Additionally, the equation relating access to vehicles should be added, along with a prohibition on self-looping. The constraints related to energy consumption, payload capacity, time constraints, and queueing should also be retained. This modification is intended to clarify the business logic; it does not aim to maintain exact equivalence to the original text.
