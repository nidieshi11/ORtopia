Retained Core Model

According to the tex formulas and the PDF, the underlying application model is the dynamic facility-location and treatment-budget allocation MIP on Page 13, but the paper states that this non-convex bilinear MIP is only practically solvable for small instances. Therefore, the retained practically solvable core model is the McCormick-envelope-based relaxation built from the Appendix/Page 27–29 formulas rather than the original non-convex main-text model. The retained model preserves the full dynamic epidemic constraints and all operational constraints, while replacing bilinear terms by standard exact/relaxed linear reformulations.

Decision variables. xi ∈ ℤ+ is the number of treatment facilities in county i, dik ≥ 0 is the treatment budget assigned to county i at time k, h ≥ 0 is the over-budget epigraph variable, zi is the reciprocal surrogate for 1∕xi used in the linearized formulation, pij,oij,rik are auxiliary variables for unary expansion and McCormick relaxations, and zk = (Sk,Pk,Ik,Ak,Rk,Dk) is the epidemic state vector.

Sets and parameters. C is the county set and K = {0,…,K} is the time-period set. The weights λD,λA,λpr,λSVI,λPop,λinf define the objective trade-off. N is the facility cap, ni is the initial number of facilities, dk is the state treatment budget at time k, dmin ⁡ is the minimum budget per facility, 𝜃 is the maximum allowed inter-period budget reduction, d is the quarterly per-patient MAT cost, and lbik,ubik are budget bounds.

Initial conditions. The six nonnegative initial compartment values are supplied data. They specify the initial population state.

(S0,P0,I0,A0,R0,D0) = (Sinit,Pinit,Iinit,Ainit,Rinit,Dinit).

Interpretation of the retained relaxation

The four inequalities (29f)–(29i) form the McCormick relaxation of rik = d¯ikzi. They define the product relaxation. The treatment term used in (28j)–(28k) is the declared relaxed expression d−1 ∑ ⁡ i(d¯ik − nirik). Solutions and optimum values refer to this relaxation with fixed initial conditions for the stated MILP relaxation.
