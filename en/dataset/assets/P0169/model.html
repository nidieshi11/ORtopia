Integrated Trading and Generation Profit Maximization


The retained formulation is the single-scenario integrated model (1)–(24), with the source-defined affine fuel-consumption functions substituted exactly. All scenario-dependent data refer to one fixed scenario.

Sets and indices

  • T ∈ ℤ>0 is the number of periods, and T = {1,…,T}.
  • Jt ∈ ℤ>0 is the number of segments in period t, and Jt = {1,…,Jt}.
  • A = {(τ,t) ∈T2 : τ ≤ t} indexes contract periods and delivery periods.
  • 𝜀 = {on,off} indexes on-peak and off-peak electricity. The supplied mapping p : ⋃ ⁡ t∈TJt → 𝜀 assigns peak status to segment index j.
  • I is the finite generator set; Gas,Coal,Nuc ⊆ I are the supplied fuel-specific generator sets.
  • D is the finite day set. For each d ∈D, t(d) ∈T is its period and j(d) ⊆Jt(d) contains its two segment indices.

All summation and constraint index ranges are integer ranges. An empty range contributes no constraints, and a sum over an empty set is zero.

Fixed parameters and boundary data

The instance supplies the following parameters. Apart from the index counts and explicitly specified discrete data, coefficients are real scalars indexed as stated.

He:

Segment duration in hours, with Hon = 16 and Hoff = 8.

P:

Regulated electricity sales price.

α:

Real-valued liquidity-limit coefficient.

Dtj,PStj:

Electricity demand and spot electricity price, respectively, for t ∈T, j ∈Jt.

PPτte,PGτt:

Electricity and gas forward prices, respectively, for (τ,t) ∈A, with e ∈ 𝜀 for electricity.

PTt:

Required profit threshold for period t ∈T.

ML:

Maximum acceptable daily loss.

CPt,NPt:

Coal and nuclear fuel prices for t ∈T.

qi,Qi:

Minimum and maximum operating generation levels for i ∈ I.

Li,li:

Supplied integer minimum up-time and down-time lengths in segment units for i ∈ I.

ai,bi:

Affine fuel-consumption intercept and slope for each i ∈Gas ∪Coal ∪Nuc. The intercept is independent of commitment status.

Witj,ϖitj ∈{0,1}:

Scheduled and forced availability indicators for i ∈ I, t ∈T, j ∈Jt; zero denotes unavailability.

Y P0te+,Y P0te−≥ 0:

Initial long and short electricity holdings for t ∈T, e ∈ 𝜀, respectively equal to the supplied initial holdings Y L0te and Y S0te.

Y G0t ≥ 0:

Initial gas holdings for t ∈T.

Uit0 ∈{0,1}:

Supplied initial operating state for each i ∈ I, t ∈T. These are fixed boundary data for each period.

FP¯τte+,FP¯τte−,Y P¯τte+,Y P¯τte−:

Supplied nonnegative upper bounds on electricity forward trade and holding components for (τ,t) ∈A, e ∈ 𝜀.

Decision variables and domains

For (τ,t) ∈A, e ∈ 𝜀, FPτte is the signed electricity forward trade and Y Pτte is the signed electricity forward holding; positive and negative values denote long and short positions. Their long and short magnitude components satisfy

FPτte,Y Pτte ∈ ℝ, (τ,t) ∈A,e ∈ 𝜀, 0 ≤ FPτte+ ≤FP¯ τte+, (τ,t) ∈A,e ∈ 𝜀, 0 ≤ FPτte− ≤FP¯ τte−, (τ,t) ∈A,e ∈ 𝜀, 0 ≤ Y Pτte+ ≤Y P¯ τte+, (τ,t) ∈A,e ∈ 𝜀, 0 ≤ Y Pτte− ≤Y P¯ τte−, (τ,t) ∈A,e ∈ 𝜀.

Gas forward purchases FGτt and holdings Y Gτt have domains

FGτt,Y Gτt ∈ ℝ≥0((τ,t) ∈A).

The remaining variables are:

  • SPtj: signed spot electricity trade, positive for purchases and negative for sales;
  • ZPte,ZGt: total electricity and gas forward costs;
  • Gitj,TGtj: individual and total generation;
  • SGtj,SCtj,SNtj: gas, coal, and nuclear fuel consumption;
  • Ctj: generation cost;
  • Uitj: operating indicator, equal to one when generator i is on.

Their domains are

ZPte ∈ ℝ t ∈T,e ∈ 𝜀, ZGt ∈ ℝ t ∈T, SPtj,TGtj,SGtj,SCtj,SNtj,Ctj ∈ ℝ t ∈T,j ∈Jt, Gitj ∈ ℝ i ∈ I,t ∈T,j ∈Jt, Uitj ∈{0,1} i ∈ I,t ∈T,j ∈Jt.

All continuous variables are real-valued and satisfy the constraints below.

Objective

Maximize over the declared decision variables:

max ⁡ ∑ t∈T ∑ j∈Jt [(DtjP − SPtjPStj)Hp(j) − Ctj] −∑ t∈T ∑ e∈𝜀ZPte. 		(24)
