Finite Scenario Investment and Operation Model


1 Sets and indexing

Let S be the finite set of damage scenarios. The distribution grid is (N,E) and the communication network is (N~,E~), including its dummy node i~d. Write E = E ∪E~ for the integrated edge set. Each edge has ordered endpoints (eh,et); δ(e) = {eh,et}, and δ(i) denotes incident distribution edges when i ∈ N. In expressions indexed by e = (i,j), i = eh and j = et. Communication incidence sums range over E~.

The existing and candidate edge sets are Ex0 and Exn, with Ex0 ∪ Exn = E. The switch sets are Et0,Etn ⊆ E, and Et = Et0 ∪ Etn. The hardening-eligible set is Eh ⊆E. Existing and candidate generators form U = U0 ∪ Un. These existing/candidate partitions are disjoint. Each generator l is located at bus i(l), and Ui = {l ∈ U : i(l) = i}. Candidate distribution lines have existing switch availability, as specified by Exn ∩ E ⊆ Et0.

Let EV ⊆ E be the transformer set, L ⊆ N the critical-load buses, and Ds ⊆E the damaged edges in scenario s. Let C be the supplied finite collection of distribution cycles and EC = ⋃ ⁡ C∈CC. Endpoint matches in the topology constraints include parallel edges.

Let P = {a,b,c}, with bus, edge, and generator phase sets Pi,Pe,Pl ⊆ P. Transformer phase sets are nonempty. Quantities on absent phases are extended by zero whenever used in a phase-indexed expression. All phase projections and extensions use this convention.

The communication control-point set is N~c ⊆N~, with mappings i~(l) ∈N~c for l ∈ U and i~(e) ∈N~c for e ∈ Et.

4.3 Lossless endpoint conservation

Endpoint flows are positive out of their own bus. Hence for every scenario, physical line e = (i,j) and phase k ∈ Pe,

pe,is,k + p e,js,k = 0,q e,is,k + q e,js,k = 0.

Distribution self-loops are invalid. Summing the nodal real-power balances now proves total generation equals total delivered demand; independent endpoint variables cannot create power.
