The indicator equalities enforce the conditional state updates exactly. Every station visit is represented once per vehicle, consistent with one arrival-state variable per node/vehicle. Positive travel/service time removes disconnected cycles; the delivery instance has distinct non-depot coordinates and positive travel on each such edge. The depot copies may be connected directly for an unused vehicle.

The source calls b and u remaining resources. Its one-sided energy rows can reduce b without physical consumption, then allow charging above true capacity. A delivered old three-vehicle certificate demonstrated this error. The new equalities and per-period bounds explicitly remove it; this specifies the physical load-dependent energy consumption. Cargo equalities remove ambiguity in the reported remaining-load values. The full source objective and all original rows are still checked. Charging availability at each station is represented by its energy and timing conditions, with simultaneous charging allowed.

The solver is Gurobi with a total per-input wall-clock budget of 3600 seconds, including any initial feasible-start search. Initial routes are warm starts only. A time-limit incumbent is accepted only after original-row, added-row, and independent exact route/energy replay checks. The result records the incumbent status and the solver bound when available.
