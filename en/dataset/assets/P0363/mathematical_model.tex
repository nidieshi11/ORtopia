\section*{Electric vehicle routes and time-dependent charge/discharge decisions}
This current model preserves all original rows and objective, and explicitly
repairs physical energy conservation. The old model and code are retained.
Source: local paper, pp.\ 4--5, Table 2 and (1)--(20).
Let $C,S$ be customer and charging-station nodes, $o=0,p$ the distinct
departure/return depot copies, $V=C\cup S\cup\{o,p\}$,
$I=C\cup S\cup\{o\}$, $J=C\cup S\cup\{p\}$, $H=S\cup\{o,p\}$.
Vehicles are $k=1,\ldots,K$, charging periods $t=1,\ldots,|T|$, length
$\delta$, and $M=\delta|T|$. Inputs are travel time $t_{ij}$, service time
$s_i$, delivery $q_i$, windows $[e_i,l_i]$, capacity $Q$, and battery
capacity $B$ in equivalent charging minutes. Travel consumes $f_{ij}$
charging minutes. If physical battery capacity is $C_b$ kWh, per-period
buy/sell costs are $P_t^{re}=\delta C_bp_t^b/B$ and
$P_t^{dis}=\delta C_bp_t^s/B$; $P_{night}=C_bp_{night}/B$.
All time/energy coefficients in the current inputs use these units.

Variables are binary route $x_{ijk}$, binary charging/discharging
$r_{itk},d_{itk}$, arrival time $\tau_{ik}\ge0$, arrival energy
$0\le b_{ik}\le B$ and arrival cargo $0\le u_{ik}\le Q$.
Define $E_{ik}=\delta\sum_t(r_{itk}-d_{itk})$ for $i\in H$ and zero
for customers.
\begin{align*}
\min\quad&
\sum_{k,i\in H,t}(r_{itk}P_t^{re}-d_{itk}P_t^{dis})
+\sum_kP_{night}(B-b_{pk}-E_{pk}).
\end{align*}
\subsection*{All routing, temporal and original resource constraints}
\begin{align}
\sum_{k,j\in J}x_{ijk}&=1 &&(i\in C),\\
\sum_{i\in I}x_{ipk}&=1 &&(k),\\
\sum_{j\in I}x_{jik}&=\sum_{j\in J}x_{ijk} &&(i\in C\cup S,k),\\
\tau_{ik}+(t_{ij}+s_i)x_{ijk}-M(1-x_{ijk})&\le\tau_{jk}&&(i\in I,j\in J,k),\\
t\delta(r_{itk}+d_{itk})+t_{ij}x_{ijk}-M(1-x_{ijk})&\le\tau_{jk}&&(i\in S\cup\{o\},j\in J,t,k),\\
e_i\le\tau_{ik}&\le l_i&&(i\in V,k),\\
b_{ok}&=B,\qquad u_{ok}=Q&&(k),\\
b_{jk}&\le b_{ik}+E_{ik}-f_{ij}x_{ijk}+B(1-x_{ijk})&&(i\in I,j\in J,k),\\
\delta\sum_t r_{itk}&\le B-b_{ik}&&(i\in H,k),\\
\delta\sum_t d_{itk}&\le b_{ik}&&(i\in H,k),\\
r_{itk}+d_{itk}&\le1&&(i\in H,t,k),\\
\tau_{ik}-(t-1)\delta&\le M(1-d_{itk}-r_{itk})&&(i\in S\cup\{p\},t,k),\\
u_{jk}&\le u_{ik}-q_ix_{ijk}+Q(1-x_{ijk})&&(i\in I,j\in J,k),\\
b_{jk}&\le B\sum_{i\in I}x_{ijk}&&(j\in S,k).
\end{align}
The original total-charge and total-discharge restrictions are retained
even though they can be more restrictive than cumulative SOC bounds.

\subsection*{Explicit physical completion}
\begin{align}
\sum_{j\in J}x_{ojk}&=1,\qquad x_{iik}=0&&(i\in C\cup S,k),\\
v_{ik}:=\sum_{j\in I\setminus\{i\}}x_{jik}&\le1&&(i\in S,k),\\
r_{itk}+d_{itk}&\le v_{ik}&&(i\in S,t,k),\\
x_{ijk}=1&\Longrightarrow b_{jk}=b_{ik}+E_{ik}-f_{ij}&&(i\in I,j\in J,k),\\
x_{ijk}=1&\Longrightarrow u_{jk}=u_{ik}-q_i&&(i\in I,j\in J,k),\\
0\le b_{ik}+\delta\sum_{a=1}^{t}(r_{iak}-d_{iak})&\le B&&(i\in H,t,k),\\
\tau_{ok}&=0&&(k).
\end{align}
The indicator equalities enforce the conditional state updates exactly. Every station visit is represented once per vehicle, consistent
with one arrival-state variable per node/vehicle. Positive travel/service
time removes disconnected cycles; the delivery instance has distinct
non-depot coordinates and positive travel on each such edge. The depot
copies may be connected directly for an unused vehicle.

The source calls $b$ and $u$ remaining resources. Its one-sided energy
rows can reduce $b$ without physical consumption, then allow charging
above true capacity. A delivered old three-vehicle certificate demonstrated
this error. The new equalities and per-period bounds explicitly remove it;
this specifies the physical load-dependent energy consumption. Cargo
equalities remove ambiguity in the reported remaining-load values.
The full source objective and all original rows are still checked.
Charging availability at each station is represented by its energy and timing conditions, with simultaneous charging allowed.

The solver is Gurobi with a total per-input wall-clock budget of 3600
seconds, including any initial feasible-start search. Initial routes are
warm starts only. A time-limit
incumbent is accepted only after original-row, added-row, and independent
exact route/energy replay checks. The result records the incumbent status and the solver bound when available.
