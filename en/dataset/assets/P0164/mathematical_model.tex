\section*{P0164 Drone charging-relay network design}
\subsection*{Mathematical formulation}
\begin{align}
\min\quad&
\frac{\theta}{\beta_1}\sum_{s\in S,i\in I,e\in E}l_e z_e^{si}
+\frac{1-\theta}{\beta_2}\sum_{k\in I}x_k,\\
\sum_{e\in\delta^+(k)}y_e^{si}-\sum_{e\in\delta^-(k)}y_e^{si}
&=\begin{cases}u_{si},&k=s,\\-u_{si},&k=i,\\0,&k\in\Gamma\setminus\{s,i\},\end{cases}
&&s\in S,\ i\in I,\ k\in\Gamma,\\
\sum_{e\in\delta^+(s)}y_e^{si}&\le1 &&s\in S,\ i\in I,\\
y_e^{si}&\le u_{si} &&s\in S,\ i\in I,\ e\in E,\\
\sum_{s\in S}u_{si}&\le1 &&i\in I,\\
\sum_{s\in S,i\in I}c_{in}u_{si}&\ge1 &&n\in N,\\
y_e^{si}&\le x_k &&s\in S,\ i\in I,\ k\in I,\ e\in\delta^-(k),\\
z_e^{si}&\le y_e^{si},\qquad z_e^{si}\le u_{si}
&&s\in S,\ i\in I,\ e\in E,\\
z_e^{si}&\ge y_e^{si}+u_{si}-1 &&s\in S,\ i\in I,\ e\in E,\\
x_k,u_{si},y_e^{si},z_e^{si}&\in\{0,1\}. &&
\end{align}

\subsection*{Definitions and model scope}
A drone operator has existing hubs, a separate collection of candidate charging stations and customer demand locations. Available directed flight connections go from hubs to candidate stations or between different candidate stations; no connection enters a hub or returns to its own origin. Every connection has already passed obstacle screening and has length no greater than twice the given flight radius. A candidate station covers a demand location exactly when its distance to that location does not exceed the flight radius. The operator chooses yes-or-no station openings $x_k$ and hub-to-terminal connections $u_{si}$, together with yes-or-no flight-connection records $y_e^{si}$ separately for every hub and terminal pair.

For each hub-terminal pair, outgoing flight records less incoming flight records equal its hub-to-terminal connection choice at that hub, the negative of that choice at the terminal, and zero at every other hub and candidate station. At most one flight connection for that pair leaves its own hub. Every flight record is at most the pair's connection choice, so none are used for an unconnected pair. Each candidate terminal is connected to at most one hub. Every demand location must be covered by at least one connected terminal, counting all hub-terminal pairs whose terminal lies within the coverage radius.

For every hub-terminal pair and every flight connection entering a candidate station, that flight record is at most the candidate station's opening record. Thus both intermediate charging stations and terminal stations entered by selected flights must be open. A station not entered may remain closed; opening alone does not make it a covering connected terminal. The design uses the full available connection network and adds no separately imposed restriction to a preselected finite list of routes.

Each pair and connection also has a yes-or-no charged flight record $z_e^{si}$. It cannot exceed either the flight record or the hub-to-terminal choice, and it must be at least their sum less one, making it precisely their simultaneous use. The operator seeks the lowest total of connection lengths weighted by these charged flight records, multiplied by the given tradeoff weight and divided by the first positive normalization constant, plus the number of opened stations multiplied by one minus the tradeoff weight and divided by the second positive normalization constant. The tradeoff weight is between zero and one. Length is counted separately for each hub-terminal pair that uses a connection.
