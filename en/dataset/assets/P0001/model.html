P1: Sample-average mixed-binary linear model for tandem clinic appointments

Source and scope Original paper, PDF pages 1, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12, equations (4)–(9), Remark 1, and Proposition 2, equations (11)–(25).

Sets, parameters, and scope

Each patient k = 1,…,K visits every single-server station n = 1,…,N in the same fixed order, and all patients attend. Ω is a given finite, equally weighted sample of service-duration scenarios. δkn(ω) ∈ ℤ+ is the number of time slots, with service times allowed to be correlated across patients and stations. Tn ∈ ℤ+ is the regular working time at the station. The cost inequalities 0 ≤ CL,k ≤ CW,k, 0 ≤ CRn ≤ CIn, and COn ≥ 0 correspond to leisure and regular waiting, usable and regular idle time, and overtime, respectively. A valid big-M value is

M = max ⁡ {1,max ⁡ nTn + max ⁡ ω∈Ω ∑ k=1K ∑ n=1Nδ kn(ω)}.

This file uses the sample-average mixed-binary linear formulation in Proposition 2. Appointment times akn are continuous. Remark 1 permits relaxing time-slot integrality, and Proposition 2 uses a continuous domain. The objective is average cost over the given sample of service durations.

Variables and complete model

a is the appointment schedule chosen in advance. In each scenario, r is the patient-ready time, v is the provider-ready time, s is the actual service start time, and f is the provider’s finish time. The binary variables y,z select the branches of the maximum relationships. ΓW ,ΓL,ΓI,ΓR,ΓO are the total durations of regular waiting, leisure waiting, regular idle time, usable idle time, and overtime, respectively. Boundary variables y,z that do not apply may be omitted.
