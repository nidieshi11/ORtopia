Explicit corrections and algorithm boundary

The source paper’s domain (18) makes Y a nonnegative integer; the old extracted binary domain was wrong. The source and extraction both print A − TkDL ≤ DL although the text defines DL as delay time. That product is represented here by the time-consistent delay bound DL ≥ A − Tk above. The delay definition uses consistent time units. The code and all rerun objectives use this corrected late-time definition. The source’s unusual global recovery implementation-time sum is retained literally.

The solver constructs an integer-flow feasible candidate on the complete network. Integer flows, at most two preparations and at most four recoveries, shortest-path choices, minimal vehicle counts and minimal modal transfer quantities are search choices only. They guide candidate construction. Every original node, arc, order and scenario remains in the certificate. The returned objective is a feasible upper bound.
