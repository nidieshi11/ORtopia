\section*{Core model: first-mile ridesharing with service-quality preferences}
Source: Sections 3.1--3.3, PDF pp.4--7, Table 2 and (1)--(19).
Only the complete original arc-based MILP is retained.
\paragraph{Data.}
$K$ is the heterogeneous vehicle set. Requests $R=R_G\mathbin{\dot\cup}R_D$
are general and high-priority groups. Vehicle $k$ starts at ready location
$g_k$ and all passengers go to station $0$. Define
\[
 A_k=\{(g_k,j):j\in R\}\cup
 \{(i,j):i\in R,j\in R\cup\{0\},i\ne j\}.
\]
All other arcs, including self-loops, outgoing station arcs and arcs to
ready locations, are absent. Vehicle capacity, ready time and latest
station arrival are $q_k,r_k,L_k$. For request $i$, $p_i$ is its number
of riders, $s_i$ boarding/service time, $e_i$ earliest pickup,
$a_i$ latest station arrival, $\varpi_i$ acceptable number of co-riders
excluding its own party, and $u_i$ acceptable ride time.
$d_{ij}$ is distance, $v>0$ speed (time units must agree), $c\ge0$
distance cost, and $f\ge0$ vehicle fixed cost.
$\alpha_g,\alpha_d$ penalize excess ride time and
$\varsigma_g,\varsigma_d$ excess co-riders; high-priority coefficients
are larger. The source uses $T=\max_i a_i$ and $P=\sum_i p_i$ in the
following deactivation terms. Its intended time horizon and distances
must make these constants valid.
\paragraph{Variables and derived assignment.}
$x^k_{ij},y^k\in\{0,1\}$ select arcs and vehicles. Let
$b_i^k:=\sum_{j\in R\cup\{0\}\setminus\{i\}}x^k_{ij}$ (an expression,
fixed expression). $dt^k\ge0$ is departure time,
$t_i^k\ge0$ pickup time ($i\in R$), and $t_0^k\ge0$ station arrival.
$\varrho^k\in\mathbb Z_+$ is total carried riders;
$\zeta_i\in\mathbb Z_+$ excess co-riders;
$\xi_i,z_i\ge0$ ride-time epigraph and excess ride-time penalty quantity.
Here $\mathbb Z_+$ includes zero.
\paragraph{One complete model.}
\begin{align}
\min\quad &\sum_{k\in K}\sum_{(i,j)\in A_k}cd_{ij}x^k_{ij}
 +\sum_{k\in K}fy^k
 +\sum_{i\in R_G}p_i(\alpha_gz_i+\varsigma_g\zeta_i)
 +\sum_{i\in R_D}p_i(\alpha_dz_i+\varsigma_d\zeta_i),\tag{1}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad&\sum_{k\in K}b_i^k=1 &&i\in R,\tag{2}\\
&\sum_{j\in(R\cup\{g_k\})\setminus\{i\}}x^k_{ji}=b_i^k
 &&i\in R,k\in K,\tag{3}\\
&y^k=\sum_{j\in R}x^k_{g_kj}=\sum_{i\in R}x^k_{i0}\le1
 &&k\in K,\tag{4}\\
&\varrho^k=\sum_{i\in R}p_i b_i^k\le q_k &&k\in K,\tag{5}\\
&\zeta_i\ge\varrho^k-p_i-\varpi_i-P(1-b_i^k)
 &&i\in R,k\in K,\tag{6}\\
&t_j^k\ge t_i^k+d_{ij}/v+s_i-T(1-x^k_{ij})
 &&i\in R,j\in(R\cup\{0\})\setminus\{i\},k\in K,\tag{7}\\
&t_j^k\ge dt^k+d_{g_kj}/v-T(1-x^k_{g_kj})
 &&j\in R,k\in K,\tag{8}\\
&e_i\le t_i^k\le a_i-d_{i0}/v-s_i &&i\in R,k\in K,\tag{9}\\
&t_0^k\le a_i+T(1-b_i^k) &&i\in R,k\in K,\tag{10}\\
&t_0^k\le L_k &&k\in K,\tag{11}\\
&\xi_i\ge t_0^k-t_i^k-T(1-b_i^k) &&i\in R,k\in K,\tag{12}\\
&z_i\ge\xi_i-u_i &&i\in R,\tag{13}\\
&dt^k\ge r_k &&k\in K,\tag{14}\\
&x^k_{ij},y^k\in\{0,1\},\quad dt^k,t_i^k,t_0^k,z_i,\xi_i\ge0,
 \quad\zeta_i,\varrho^k\in\mathbb Z_+ .\tag{15--19}
\end{align}
The hard seat capacity is never softened. Co-rider and ride-time
preferences are soft, but the latest station arrival remains hard for
\emph{every} assigned request. With nonnegative penalty coefficients the
epigraph variables attain their relevant positive-part quantities at an
optimal solution.
