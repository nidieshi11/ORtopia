\section*{Core model: minimum-cost JOCR-R}
Source: Sections 3 and 4.1, PDF pp.5--7 (printed pp.624--626),
equations (1)--(12). The restricted-focus and unrestricted-focus policies
are both introduced as main models in Section 4. The retained policy is
JOCR-R, matching the discrete-location input and stated code interface.
\paragraph{Scope and data.}
$L=\{0,1,\ldots,N\}$ contains the depot $0$ and customer locations.
Focal points must be in $L$. A truck carries at most $G$ identical drones;
each drone makes at most one out-and-back delivery per cluster and
returns to the waiting truck. The truck can carry all orders; it visits
all selected focal points in one tour. $K\ge2$ is the maximum number
of selected focal points, including the depot. For each location $i$,
$(A_i,B_i)$ is its coordinate and $F_i\ge0$ its payload-dependent
\emph{one-way admissible assignment distance}, in the convention of (8).
Truck-only locations have $F_i=0$. Distinct location coordinates ensure
this forces them to be focal points. Define
\[
 D^E_{ij}=\sqrt{(A_i-A_j)^2+(B_i-B_j)^2},\qquad
 D^R_{ij}=|A_i-A_j|+|B_i-B_j|.
\]
$C_{D_1},C_{D_2},C_T\ge0$ are fixed drone cost, drone distance cost,
and truck distance cost. A round-trip flight contributes $2D^E_{ij}$.
\paragraph{Variables.}
$x_{ij}\in\{0,1\}$ assigns location $i$ to focal point $j$;
$x_{jj}=1$ selects $j$. $y_{ij}\in\{0,1\}$ for $i\ne j$ selects
truck arc $i\to j$; $y_{ii}=0$.
$g\in\{0,\ldots,G\}$ is the drone count. Nondepot order variables
$u_i\in\{1,\ldots,K-1\}$ represent an ordering; unselected locations
can take any value in that range. Their integrality and bounded
representatives do not remove any valid tour with at most $K$ focal points.
\paragraph{Single complete cost model.}
\begin{align}
\min\quad &C_{D_1}g+2C_{D_2}\sum_{i\in L}\sum_{j\in L}D^E_{ij}x_{ij}
 +C_T\sum_{i\in L}\sum_{j\in L\setminus\{i\}}D^R_{ij}y_{ij},\tag{1}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad &\sum_{j\in L}x_{jj}\le K,\tag{2}\\
&x_{ij}\le x_{jj} &&i,j\in L,\tag{3}\\
&\sum_{j\in L}x_{ij}=1 &&i\in L,\tag{4}\\
&x_{00}=1,\tag{5}\\
&\sum_{i\in L}x_{ij}\le g+1 &&j\in L,\tag{6}\\
&0\le g\le G,\quad g\in\mathbb Z,\tag{7}\\
&D^E_{ij}x_{ij}\le F_i &&i,j\in L,\tag{8}\\
&\sum_{j\in L\setminus\{i\}}y_{ij}=x_{ii} &&i\in L,\tag{9}\\
&\sum_{i\in L\setminus\{j\}}y_{ij}=x_{jj} &&j\in L,\tag{10}\\
&u_i-u_j+(K-1)y_{ij}+(K-3)y_{ji}\le K-2
 &&i,j\in L\setminus\{0\},i\ne j,\tag{11}\\
&x_{ij}\in\{0,1\},\quad y_{ij}\in\{0,1\},\quad
 u_i\in\{1,\ldots,K-1\}.\tag{12}
\end{align}
The coefficients in (11) are transcribed from the rendered source page,
with the cardinalities displayed in those equations.
Cluster cardinality in (6) includes its focal location; the remaining
customers require at most $g$ parallel drones.

\paragraph{Published-formulation boundary.}
The degree equations (9)--(10) impose a nontrivial truck tour, hence at
least two selected focal points.
Unrestricted focal coordinates, completion-time objectives, epsilon
trade-offs and multiple deliveries by one drone per cluster are excluded.
