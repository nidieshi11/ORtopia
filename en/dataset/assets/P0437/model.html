The source defines Psurg = ln ⁡ (Nsurg) and Pq,∞ = ln ⁡ (Nq,∞). Here Nsurg is the fixed clinically determined operable size and 𝜖 is the fixed probability of not meeting the target. The superscript (k) denotes the value under scenario k; the treatment variables U,C,E are shared across scenarios. The objective minimizes terminal log populations for scenario 1, not an expectation over scenarios.
