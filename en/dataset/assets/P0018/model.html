Retained Core Model

According to the tex formulas and the PDF, the retained directly solvable core model is the compact MILP reformulation R-MILP for the case M = 2, given by Theorem 3 (Pages 14 and 18). The earlier formulations B-IFP and R-BFP are omitted because they are fractional/nonlinear intermediate models, while the later outer-approximation algorithms are solution methods rather than the core model itself.

Decision variables. xj ∈{0,1} opens drone base j, yij ∈{0,1} assigns overdose request location i to base j, and γj(m) ∈{0,1} indicates whether exactly m drones are positioned at base j for m = 1,2. The remaining variables Uj(1),Uj(2),zjlt,μlj(m),τjlt,ωij(m) are standard auxiliary variables introduced by the exact linearization. Let Ūj(m) be a valid upper bound on Uj(m), and let μ¯ij(1) be a valid upper bound on μij(1).
