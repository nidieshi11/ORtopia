Basis and scope of the revision

Restoring the conservation of energy throughout the entire network by taking into account the inflow and outflow values on page 21 of the original text. This approach also helps to address issues related to limited procurement/transportation capabilities, as well as limitations in the training domain of the predictor. The local nutrition distribution model provides a framework for managing limited business boundaries.

Retained Core Model

The World Food Programme food-basket model on page 21 embeds a learned nutritional constraint in a mixed-integer optimization problem. It is directly solvable after the predictive function ĥ(x) is instantiated by any trained MIO-representable learner supported by the paper (e.g., linear model, tree, forest, or ReLU network).

Decision variables. xk denotes the quantity of food item k, Fijk is the commodity flow variable on the supply/transportation network, and y is the predicted palatability score (or predicted learned constraint output) produced by the embedded model ĥ(x).

Sets and parameters. NS, NT, and ND are source, transshipment, and demand-node sets. K is the commodity set and L is the nutrient set. The costs pikP and pijkT are procurement and transportation costs, γ is a conversion factor, di is daily demand, days is the number of planning days, Nutvalkl is the nutrient content of food k for nutrient l, Nutreql is the required amount of nutrient l, and t is the minimum acceptable learned-score threshold. To make the retained model fully solvable, we instantiate the learned mapping ĥ(x) with a trained ReLU network, which is one of the MIO-representable learners explicitly supported by the paper. Let Rℓ be the neuron set of hidden layer ℓ = 1,…,H, with trained weights Wrq(ℓ), biases br(ℓ), and valid pre-activation bounds Lr(ℓ) ≤ sr(ℓ) ≤ Ur(ℓ).

Interpretation

The application-level model on Page 21 becomes a complete standalone MIO once constraint (6h) is replaced by the explicit predictor embedding above. The paper supports other MIO-representable learners as well, but the trained ReLU network is a fully specified retained instantiation and therefore closes the previously missing learned-constraint block.
