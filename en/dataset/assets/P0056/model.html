Approximation and solver limits

The model approximates quantum evolution by the first-order Euler recurrence with continuous controls. The trace-based loss is real-valued and may be negative. Channel-sum deviations contribute a squared penalty. State feasibility is determined by the recurrence and the displayed variable domains.

Six reproducible bounded local-search starts return the best feasible point within a monotonic 3600-second budget; convergence may end the computation earlier. The solver reports the best feasible local-search point. A separate real/imaginary recurrence independently checks all states, control bounds, losses, CVaR epigraph/value, penalty, and objective. Unitarity deviation is recorded as a diagnostic.
