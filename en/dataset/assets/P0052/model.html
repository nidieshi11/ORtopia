Robust operational meaning

The optimization uses the upper-consumption trajectory. Under any smaller realized nonnegative leg consumptions, charge no more than the planned cell power, curtailing at SOCmaxCr. If actual arrival energy is a and the planned cell energy is q, the feedback update is a+ = min ⁡ {SOCmaxCr,a + q}. Induction gives actual energy at least the upper-consumption trajectory and at most the SOC ceiling; resource use and cost do not increase. This curtailment policy provides a feasible operating policy and a conservative cost upper bound.
