\section*{This is a **integrated optimization problem** that combines two components: – **Tail Assignment Problem** (TAP) – **Cargo Routing Problem** (CRP) The problem is modeled as a **mixed integer linear programming problem** (MILP). The paper proposes two models: a connection-based model and a route-based model.}

\textbf{ Model source:}: Derived from calculations

\subsection*{Decision variables}
\[
\begin{aligned}
& z_{bp} \in \mathbb{Z}^+, && \forall b \in B, \forall p \in P \\
& v_b \in \mathbb{Z}^+, && \forall b \in B
\end{aligned}
\]

\subsection*{Objective function}
\[
\max \quad \sum_{b \in B} \sum_{p \in P} (q_b - c_{bp}) z_{bp} - \sum_{b \in B} c_b v_b
\]

\subsection*{Constraints}
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{p \in P} z_{bp} + v_b = D_b, && \forall b \in B \\
&\sum_{b \in B} \sum_{p \in P_i} z_{bp} \le Cap_i, && \forall i \in L
\end{aligned}
\]

\subsection*{Complete mathematical model}
\[
\begin{aligned}
\max \quad & \sum_{b \in B} \sum_{p \in P} (q_b - c_{bp}) z_{bp} - \sum_{b \in B} c_b v_b \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{p \in P} z_{bp} + v_b = D_b, && \forall b \in B \\
& \sum_{b \in B} \sum_{p \in P_i} z_{bp} \le Cap_i, && \forall i \in L \\
& z_{bp}, v_b \in \mathbb{Z}^+, && \forall b \in B, \forall p \in P
\end{aligned}
\]
