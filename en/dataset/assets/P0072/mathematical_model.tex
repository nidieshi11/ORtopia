\section*{P0072 Two-Echelon Customer Deliveries by Trucks Carrying Drones}
\subsection*{Mathematical formulation}
\[
\begin{aligned}
&x^k_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} && \forall (i,j) \in A, k \in K^v \\
&y^k_d \in \{0, 1\} && \forall k \in K^v, d \in K^d \\
&h^k_i \in \{0, 1\} && \forall i \in Z, k \in K^v \\
&u_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} && \forall i \in Z, j \in Z^d \\
&z_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} && \forall i \in Z, j \in Z^d \\
&\omega^k_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in Z, k \in K^v \\
&a_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in V \\
&\varphi_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in Z
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\min\quad\sum_{(i,j)\in A}\sum_{k\in K^v}t^v_{ij}x^k_{ij}
+\sum_{i\in Z}\varphi_i
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{i \in V} \sum_{k \in K^v} x^k_{ij} + \sum_{i \in Z} u_{ij} = 1 && \forall j \in Z, \\
&\sum_{j \in Z} x^k_{0j} \le 1 && \forall k \in K^v, \\
&\sum_{i \in V} x^k_{ij} = \sum_{i \in V} x^k_{ji} && \forall j \in V, k \in K^v, \\
&u_{ij} \le \sum_{k \in K^v} h^k_i && \forall i \in Z, j \in Z^d, \\
&h^k_i \le \sum_{d \in K^d} y^k_d && \forall i \in Z, k \in K^v, \\
&h^k_i \le \sum_{j \in V} x^k_{ji} && \forall i \in Z, k \in K^v, \\
&\sum_{d \in K^d} y^k_d \le \Gamma && \forall k \in K^v, \\
&u_{ij} \le E_{ij} && \forall i \in Z, j \in Z^d, \\
&\sum_{i \in Z} u_{ij} = \sum_{i \in Z} z_{ij} && \forall j \in Z^d, \\
&z_{ij} + \sum_{k \in K^v} h^k_i \le 1 + u_{ij} && \forall i \in Z^d, j \in Z^d, \\
&z_{ij} \le u_{ij} && \forall i \in Z \setminus Z^d, j \in Z^d, \\
&u_{li} - u_{lj} \le 1 + \sum_{k \in K^v} h^k_i - z_{ij} && \forall i \in Z^d, j \in Z^d, l \in Z, \\
&z_{ij} \le \sum_{l \in Z} u_{li} + \sum_{k \in K^v} h^k_i && \forall i \in Z^d, j \in Z^d, \\
&\sum_{j \in Z^d} z_{ij} \le \sum_{d \in K^d} y^k_d + M(1 - h^k_i) && \forall i \in Z, k \in K^v, \\
&\sum_{j \in Z^d} z_{ij} \le 1 + M' \sum_{k \in K^v} h^k_i && \forall i \in Z, \\
&q_j + \sum_{i \in Z^d} q_i u_{ji} - \omega^k_j \le M_j(1 - \sum_{i \in V} x^k_{ij}) && \forall j \in Z, k \in K^v, \\
&\sum_{j \in Z} \omega^k_j + q^d_1 \sum_{d \in K^d} y^k_d \le Q && \forall k \in K^v, \\
&t^v_{0j} - a_j \le M'_j(1 - \sum_{k \in K^v} x^k_{0j}) && \forall j \in Z, \\
&a_i + \varphi_i + t^v_{ij} - a_j \le M_{ij}(1 - \sum_{k \in K^v} x^k_{ij}) && \forall i \in Z, j \in V, \\
&a_i + t_0 + t^d_{ij} - a_j \le M'_{ij}(2 - z_{ij} - u_{ij}) && \forall i \in Z, j \in Z^d, \\
&a_i + s^d_i + t^d_{il} + t_0 + t^d_{lj} - a_j \le M_{ijl}(2 - z_{ij} - u_{li}) && \forall l \in Z, i \in Z^d, j \in Z^d, \\
&\varphi_i \ge s^v_i \sum_{j \in V} \sum_{k \in K^v} x^k_{ji} && \forall i \in Z, \\
&a_j + s^d_j + t^d_{ji} - a_i - \varphi_i \le M''_{ij}(1 - u_{ij}) && \forall i \in Z, j \in Z^d, \\
&a_i \le l_i && \forall i \in V, \\
&\sum_{k\in K^v}y_d^k\le 1 && \forall d\in K^d.
\end{aligned}

\subsection*{Definitions and model scope}
A distributor serves customers $Z$ from a single depot $0$, with all nodes in $V$ and allowed directed arcs $A$ excluding self-loops. Physical trucks $K^v$ carry drones from the global physical fleet $K^d$, up to count $\Gamma$ per truck. Drone-eligible customers belong to $Z^d$. Truck-arc choices $x_{ij}^k$, drone assignments $y_d^k$, truck launch-site records $h_i^k$, dispatches $u_{ij}$ from stopping customers to drone customers and drone-task succession records $z_{ij}$ are zero or one. Missing truck arcs and dispatches to ineligible customers are zero. Each customer's total truck arrivals plus all drone dispatches equals exactly one. Each truck has at most one departure from depot to customers and equal incoming and outgoing counts at every node, including depot.

A dispatch from a stopping customer to a drone customer requires a truck's launch-site record at that stop. Each truck's launch-site record cannot exceed its assigned drone count or its incoming-arc count there. Each truck carries no more than the allowed drone count, and each physical drone's assignments across trucks sum to at most one. Dispatch also cannot exceed round-trip energy-feasibility indicator $E_{ij}$.

For each drone customer, all incoming dispatches must total the same as all incoming task-succession records. When a current node is drone-eligible, its succession record to a next drone customer plus all launch-site records at the current node cannot exceed one plus its dispatch record to that next customer. When the current node is ineligible, succession cannot exceed the corresponding dispatch. For any two drone customers and any stopping customer, dispatch from the stop to the first minus dispatch to the second cannot exceed one plus all launch-site records at the first customer minus succession from first to second. That succession record also cannot exceed all dispatches received by the first customer plus its launch-site records.

For every node and truck, total outgoing drone-task succession cannot exceed that truck's drone count plus valid allowance $M$ times one minus the truck's launch-site record there. It also cannot exceed one plus another valid allowance $M'$ times all launch-site records there. Allowances are explicitly supplied; residual upper bounds when a record is inactive are retained.

Customer demands $q_i$, weight of each drone and supporting equipment $q_1^d$ and truck capacity $Q$ are supplied. Each truck-customer load-accounting upper amount $\omega_i^k$ is nonnegative. Own customer demand plus total demand dispatched by drones from that customer, minus the accounting amount, cannot exceed customer allowance $M_j$ times one minus that truck's incoming-arc count. Each truck's accounting amounts summed over all customers plus assigned drone count times equipment weight cannot exceed capacity. Accounting amounts at unvisited customers are not forced to zero.

Service-start records $a_i$ are nonnegative at every node; the depot record is a common return upper time rather than departure time. Nonnegative customer stopping duration $\varphi_i$ includes truck service and waiting for drones. Depot-to-customer driving time $t_{0j}^v$ minus customer start cannot exceed allowance $M'_j$ times one minus the total truck departures directly to that customer. For a truck arc from a customer to any node, current start plus stopping duration plus driving time $t_{ij}^v$ minus next-node start cannot exceed allowance $M_{ij}$ times one minus total truck selections of that arc.

From every stopping customer to every drone customer, current start plus launch preparation $t_0$ and flight time $t_{ij}^d$ minus customer start cannot exceed allowance $M'_{ij}$ multiplied by two minus the succession record minus the dispatch record. For every stop and two drone customers, first-customer start plus its service duration $s_i^d$, flight back to the stop, preparation and flight from the stop to the second customer, minus second-customer start, cannot exceed allowance $M_{ijl}$ multiplied by two minus succession between the customers minus dispatch from the stop to the first.

Each stopping duration must cover truck service time $s_i^v$ times all truck arrivals at that customer. For each stop and drone customer, customer start plus its service and flight back to the stop, minus stop start and stopping duration, cannot exceed allowance $M''_{ij}$ times one minus dispatch. Every node's start cannot exceed its latest limit $l_i$, with depot limit $l_0$ ending the planning horizon. The distributor seeks the lowest sum of driving time on all selected truck arcs and stopping duration across all customers. Stopping includes service rather than only waiting for drones.
