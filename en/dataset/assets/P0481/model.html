Sets, fixed inputs, and variable roles

The scheduling horizon is T, with timeslot index t; W is the scenario set, with index w; and ρw is the probability of scenario w. The aggregator set is G, indexed by g in the equations (the nomenclature also uses k). The transit-node and APDN-node sets are N and B, respectively. The source defines

I = O ∪S ∪D,OS = O ∪S,SD = D ∪S,

where O contains first trips from the origin depot, S the service trips excluding the first and last depot trips, and D the last trips to the destination depot. Indices i,j denote trips and o a first depot trip. Charging nodes and aggregator-managed nodes satisfy

NB ⊂N,BN ⊂B,BG ⊂B.

The charging nodes NB connect to the APDN through BN; BG denotes APDN nodes managed by DR aggregators. The APDN is the only source of BEB charging power. The APDN is radial, and b and b + 1 are described as exclusive sequential nodes. One load is assumed at each APDN node.

Connecting the original source definitions on these benchmarks. The stage-one solution is fixed before charging. A service connection has one physical terminal, mapped according to the terminal field in the input. For a selected pair i,j the index n in (12),(15),(16) denotes the data-mapped physical terminal after service i; a diagonal trip connection has value zero. At a station the associated en-route and depot charges add to its nodal electricity demand, as described by the bus-to-APDN connection in §§3.1 and 5.1. The periodic physical depot opportunity of each bus uses its own first service f and last service l. The bus returns at R = ūl + τlS2D and the next day departs for f at 1440 + D, D = u̲f − τfO2S. Map the closed interval [R,1440 + D] modulo 1440 to the supplied two-minute grid. All energy bought there is credited once to this bus between its return and its next full departure, including predeparture minutes after midnight when available. If D < 0 the depot opportunity ends before the next midnight. The terminal window for a selected successive pair remains its actual inter-trip interval. Equation (23) prints incomplete endpoints, so the finite tail-only variant and the nonphysical relaxation imposing no zero power off those opportunities are also certified separately. The latter uses nonnegative power with the printed in-window limits. Its value is meaningful only for that mathematical branch. Each chain, physical opportunity, station, time and energy is available in the generator and solution. The branch equations are used on every physical line, with the downstream net demand as the terminal boundary of the radial chain. The two reported capacity regimes are the per-connection ratings of (22)–(23) and the additional shared station nameplates of §5.1. They can give different costs.

This leaves net demand and unserved energy unchanged, attains zero follower cost and reduces the upper objective without bound.
