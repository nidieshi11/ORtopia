1 Sets and Parameters

Let Ng, Nd, M, and Nl be finite sets of generators, demand nodes, buses, and transmission lines, respectively, with cardinalities ng, nd, nb, and nl. Let T be the number of periods and T = {1,…,T} the ordered planning horizon.

The complete vertex array has dimension R × nd × T and defines the uncertainty polytope, including lower-dimensional polytopes.

For each i ∈Ng, the supplied scalar parameters are:
