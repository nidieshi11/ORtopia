\section*{P0145 Collaborative Deliveries with One Drone per Truck under Fixed Demand}
\subsection*{Mathematical formulation}
\[
\sum_k(v_{jk}+b_{jk})\le1,\quad h_k\le1,\quad
\sum_ix_{ifk}=h_k,\quad v_{jk}=\sum_lx_{jlk},\quad a_{ok}=u_{ok}=0.
\]

\[
a_{jk}\le Hv_{jk},\quad a'_{jk}\le Hb_{jk},\quad
L_{jk}\ge a_{jk}-D_j,\quad L_{jk}\ge a'_{jk}-D_j,\quad
\sum_jd_j(v_{jk}+b_{jk})\le Q_T.
\]

\[
r_{ijk}\le x_{ijk},\quad
a_{jk}\ge a_{ik}+t_{ij}-M(1-x_{ijk}),\quad
u_{jk}\ge u_{ik}+1-|V|(1-x_{ijk}).
\]

\[
a'_{jk}\ge a_{ik}+t'_{ij}-M(1-y_{ijlk}),\quad
a_{lk}\ge a'_{jk}+t'_{jl}-M(1-y_{ijlk}),\quad
u_{lk}\ge u_{ik}+1-|V|(1-y_{ijlk}).
\]

\[
\min c_T\sum_{ijk}t_{ij}x_{ijk}
+c_D\sum_{(i,j,l),k}(t'_{ij}+t'_{jl})y_{ijlk}
+\sum_{jk}\alpha_jL_{jk}
+\sum_j\beta_j(1-\sum_k(v_{jk}+b_{jk})).
\]

\subsection*{Definitions and model scope}
A distributor assigns vehicles $K$ to currently known customers $C$, with one drone per vehicle, departing from source $o$ and ending at sink $f$. Allowed truck arcs $E$ run from source or customers to customers or sink and exclude self-loops. Allowed complete flights $F$ contain three distinct nodes: launch, served customer and recovery. The customer must be air-service eligible, its demand $d_j$ cannot exceed drone capacity $Q_D$, and flight durations $t'_{ij}$ plus $t'_{jl}$ cannot exceed endurance $E_D$. Planning uses this fixed demand snapshot rather than anticipating or simulating later orders.

Truck movement $x_{ijk}$, drone carriage $r_{ijk}$ and complete flights $y_{ijlk}$ take zero or one. Truck customer-visit record $v_{jk}$ is that vehicle's incoming-arc count. Drone-service record $b_{jk}$ totals complete flights serving that customer as their middle node. For each customer, both service types summed over vehicles total at most one; rejection is allowed at a charge. Vehicle-use record $h_k$ equals source departures and is at most one. Sink arrivals equal that record, and truck arrivals and departures balance at each customer.

Truck arrival $a_{ik}$ and drone-customer arrival $a'_{jk}$ lie between zero and horizon $H$, with truck source time zero. Customer truck time cannot exceed horizon times truck-visit record, and drone time cannot exceed horizon times drone-service record, so unserved times of that kind are zero. Nonnegative lateness $L_{jk}$ must cover both truck time minus deadline $D_j$ and drone time minus that deadline. Total demand served by either truck or drone on each vehicle cannot exceed truck delivery capacity $Q_T$.

Drone-carriage records cannot exceed corresponding truck-arc selections. On every truck arc, destination arrival is at least preceding arrival plus driving time $t_{ij}$ minus valid allowance $M$ times one minus arc use. Node-order record $u_{ik}$ lies between zero and total node count, with source order zero. Destination order is at least preceding order plus one minus total node count times one minus arc choice. These finite allowances remain on unselected arcs.

Each vehicle has at most one launch and one recovery at any node. At source and sink these counts also cannot exceed vehicle use, and at customers they cannot exceed truck visits. For each complete flight, drone-customer arrival is at least launch-node truck arrival plus first flight duration minus allowance times one minus flight choice. Recovery-node truck arrival is at least drone-customer arrival plus second flight duration minus the same allowance. Recovery order is at least launch order plus one minus total node count times one minus flight choice.

For each vehicle, drone-carriage arcs and complete flights linking launch to recovery jointly form a continuous source-to-sink flow. Outgoing minus incoming flow equals vehicle use at source, its negative at sink and zero elsewhere. Together with strictly increasing truck order, this prevents overlapping flights by the same drone. Total cost should be as low as possible: truck time rate $c_T$ times all selected truck travel, drone time rate $c_D$ times both legs of selected flights, customer-specific lateness rate $\alpha_j$ times every customer-vehicle lateness record, and rejection fee $\beta_j$ times one minus total service of each customer. Rejected customers cannot be counted as delivered.

\subsection*{Inline notation and relations}
$C$; $K$; $o,f$; $E\subset(\{o\}\cup C)\times(C\cup\{f\})$; $F$; $(i,j,l)$; $j$; $d_j\le Q_D$; $t'_{ij}+t'_{jl}\le E_D$; $x_{ijk},r_{ijk},y_{ijlk}$; $a_{ik},a'_{jk}\in[0,H]$; $u_{ik}\in[0,|V|]$; $L_{jk}\ge0$; $v_{jk}=\sum_i x_{ijk}$; $b_{jk}=\sum_{(i,j,l)\in F}y_{ijlk}$; $h_k=\sum_jx_{ojk}$; $h_k$; $v_{jk}$; $r$; $y$; $o$; $f$; $-h_k$.
