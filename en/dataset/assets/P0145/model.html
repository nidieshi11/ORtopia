P145: Current complete mathematical model (as of 2026-10-20)

Basis and scope of the revision

Restored limited delivery capacity per vehicle, achievable range, and continuous delivery of individual drones. The combined path model involving trucks and drones, based on fixed-demand snapshots, enables optimal optimization of delivery routes.

Data and variables

Let C be the set of customers, K the set of vehicles, and o,f the source and sink. The allowed truck arcs E ⊂ ({o}∪ C) × (C ∪{f}) contain no self-loops. Each vehicle carries one drone. The set F consists of allowable sorties (i,j,l) with three distinct nodes and a drone-eligible customer j, satisfying dj ≤ QD and tij′ + tjl′≤ ED. Capacity, time, deadlines, costs, and rejection penalties are instance parameters. The binary variables xijk,rijk,yijlk represent truck movement, a drone traveling on the truck, and a complete drone sortie, respectively. Time variables satisfy aik,ajk′∈ [0,H], the ordering variable is uik ∈ [0,|V |], and lateness is Ljk ≥ 0. Define vjk = ∑ ⁡ ixijk, bjk = ∑ ⁡ (i,j,l)∈F yijlk, and hk = ∑ ⁡ jxojk.

For each vehicle and each node, the drone follows a path consisting of an arc r along with a complete flight path y. This combined path forms a unit flow from o to f. The difference between outflow and inflow is hk at o, − hk at f, and zero for all other cases. The order in which trucks are processed is determined by this flow, ensuring that no overlapping flights occur.
