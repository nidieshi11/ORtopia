Zero diagonal and the exact counting scope of (15)

For notational purposes, define xi,ik = 0 for every node and vehicle, and di,i = 0. These are fixed self-arc constants. All all-node sums therefore have the same value as sums over distinct nodes. In (15), the second sum excludes both i and i + n: exclusion of i is present in the paper’s printed formula, and exclusion of i + n merely removes its zero self-arc. Together with the visit and flow requirements this forbids a direct selected arc from a pickup to its own paired delivery. This retained formulation therefore imposes the stated counting restriction in addition to its vehicle-pairing purpose.
