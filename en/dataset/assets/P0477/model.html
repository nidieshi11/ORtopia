The selected formulation retains the negative −μ^μ^T ∙ Qd(t) and the coefficients γ4,γ3 of (68). Earlier (65) instead prints a positive quadratic mean term and uses γ1,γ2; those are not the selected objective. Likewise (69) retains both 1 2qd(t) and qd(t) = −pd(t) − Qd(t)μ^, although the off-diagonal term in (64) lacks that one-half scaling. The selected final SDP retains this one-half factor.

The selected matrix initial condition is (73). Upstream scalar equations (8) and (18) print v(k) = lobs(k); this is not used to redefine the abstract vector xobs(k). A missing lower-bound row is not silently added to the selected printed SDP.
