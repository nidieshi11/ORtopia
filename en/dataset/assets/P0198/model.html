Binary Set-Partitioning Formulation for s-Club Partitioning


Notation and Input Data

Let G = (V,E) be a finite undirected graph, let s be a positive integer, and let S denote the complete finite family of admissible s-clubs, represented as subsets of V . Thus,

S ⊆ 2V ,

and every membership incidence (i,S) with i ∈ V and S ∈S is supplied as input. Equivalently, when the family is generated from G and s,

S = {S ⊆ V : diam ⁡ (G[S]) ≤ s},

where G[S] is the subgraph induced by S and the diameter is measured using shortest-path distances in G[S].

Decision Variables

For each S ∈S, let

λS ∈{0,1},

where λS = 1 indicates that the set S is selected in the partition.

Binary Set-Partitioning Model

min ⁡ ∑ S∈SλS (2a) s.t. ∑ S∈S:i∈SλS = 1 ∀ ⁡i ∈ V, (2b) λS ∈{0,1} ∀ ⁡S ∈S. (2c)
