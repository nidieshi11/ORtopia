\section*{Capacitated vehicle routing: a single route model}
Given an undirected graph $G=(V,E)$ with $V=\{0,1,\ldots,n\}$ and
$V_c=V\setminus\{0\}$, node $0$ is the depot. Edge costs $d_{ij}\ge0$
and demands $0\le q_i\le\bar q$ are inputs; $q_0=0$, and $m$ is the
available fleet size. Each undirected edge may be traversed in either direction.
Select $m_s\in\{0,\ldots,m\}$ nonempty routes. A route is an ordered
sequence $R_i^s=(0,v_1^i,\ldots,v_{t_i-1}^i,0)$ with $t_i\ge2$;
its internal nodes are distinct customers. Write $C_i$ for its customer set.
For $n>0$ the customer partition below implies $m_s\ge1$.
\begin{align*}
\min\quad &\sum_{i=1}^{m_s}\sum_{h=0}^{t_i-1}d_{v_h^i v_{h+1}^i}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad &v_0^i=v_{t_i}^i=0 && i=1,\ldots,m_s,\\
&v_h^i\in V_c &&i=1,\ldots,m_s,\ h=1,\ldots,t_i-1,\\
&v_h^i\ne v_{h'}^i &&1\le h<h'<t_i,\ i=1,\ldots,m_s,\\
&\{v_h^i,v_{h+1}^i\}\in E &&i=1,\ldots,m_s,\ h=0,\ldots,t_i-1,\\
&C_i=\{v_h^i:1\le h<t_i\},\quad C_i\cap C_j=\varnothing &&i\ne j,\\
&\bigcup_{i=1}^{m_s}C_i=V_c,\\
&\sum_{v\in C_i}q_v\le\bar q &&i=1,\ldots,m_s,\\
&m_s\le m.
\end{align*}
This makes explicit the no-node-repetition convention in Section 2, PDF page 3,
of the AILS-II paper. Only the start and terminal depot coincide. Unused vehicles
need no empty route.
