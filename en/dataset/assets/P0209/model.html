N − 1-secure day-ahead flow-based market coupling with exact projection


Source PDF pages 50–51 and 54–55, (EC.3)–(EC.6) and (EC.22)–(EC.29). Only day-ahead clearing is retained.

Sets, mappings, and parameters

Let N be the set of nodes, Z the set of zones, C the set of consumers, R the set of reserve control areas, and G = GSLOW ∪˙ GFAST the set of generators. Generator g has production bids I(g), with I = ⋃ ˙g∈GI(g). The node and zone mappings are n(g),z(g),n(c),z(c), and G(r) = {g : r(g) = r}. The sets GSLOW(a), I(a), and C(a) contain the corresponding generators, bids, and consumers at node or zone a.

The day-ahead parameters are Kg,QgL,QgR,QgU,Pi,Qi,Pc,Qc,Pr,Qr, together with a valid source-prescribed Mg for every g ∈ GSLOW. Let F = F(PN−1FB−EP) be the complete finite facet family of the exact projected N − 1 flow-based domain, with supplied coefficients V f,z and Wf. This projection is constructed while ignoring slow-generator technical minima.

Day-ahead FBMC clearing

The decision variables are ug,vi,wg,xc,yr,pz,σg,ςi,τg,νg,ρz,πr,ϕ, and γf. Their domains include

ug ∈{0,1}(g ∈ GSLOW),v i ≥ 0(i ∈ I(g),g ∈ GSLOW),w g ≥ 0(g ∈ GSLOW),
0 ≤ vi ≤ 1,0 ≤ wg ≤ 1(g ∈ GFAST),0 ≤ x c ≤ 1,σc ≥ 0(c ∈ C),
0 ≤ yr ≤ 1,σr ≥ 0,πr ≥ 0(r ∈ R),σg,τg,νg,ςi ≥ 0,

with pz,ρz,ϕ ∈ ℝ and γf ≥ 0.

The day-ahead problem is

min ⁡ ∑ g∈GSLOWKgug + ∑ i∈IPiQivi + ∑ c∈CPcQcxc + ∑ r∈RPrQryr. (EC.22)

Subject to, for slow generators,

vi ≤ ug (g ∈ GSLOW,i ∈ I(g)), (1)  wg ≤ ug (g ∈ GSLOW), (2)  QgLu g + ∑ i∈I(g)Qivi + QgRw g ≤ QgU (g ∈ GSLOW), (3)  σg ≥ QgLρ z(g) + ∑ i∈I(g)ςi + τg − QgLν g − Kg − Mg(1 − ug) (g ∈ GSLOW). (4) 

For all generators and fast-generator capacity,

ςi ≥ Qi(ρz(g) − νg − Pi) (g ∈ G,i ∈ I(g)), (5) τg ≥ QgR(π r(g) − νg) (g ∈ G), (6) ∑ i∈I(g)Qivi + QgRw g ≤ QgU (g ∈ GFAST). (7)

For consumers and reserves,

σc ≥ Qc(ρz(c) − Pc) (c ∈ C), (8) ∑ g∈G(r)QgRw g ≥ Qr(1 − yr) (r ∈ R), (9) σr ≥ Qr(πr − Pr) (r ∈ R). (10)

Economic meanings and input obligations

Kg is the slow-generator parent block cost; QgL,QgR,QgU are minimum production, reserve offer and total capacity. Pi,Qi denote production bid price and quantity. Pc,Qc and Pr,Qr describe consumer and reserve-response bids. u accepts the parent block, v,w accept production/reserve fractions, and x,y accept demand/reserve response fractions. p denotes zonal net export. ρ,π are zonal energy and reserve prices. The nonnegative σ,ς,τ,ν,γ and free ϕ support European pricing and the associated surplus relations. The complete facet coefficients (V,W) and valid Mg must be supplied with their projected-domain definitions. The full projected domain is supplied as input. The minus sign on the fast-generator surplus inside the parenthesis in (EC.29) is retained as printed in the cited zonal formulation.
