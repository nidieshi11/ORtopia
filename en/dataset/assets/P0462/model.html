CFR full-route set-partitioning model

The objective minimizes total transportation cost. The decision variable yr equals one if route r is selected and zero otherwise. Equations (5) require every customer to be visited exactly once. The set R is the full set of feasible routes defined below, not a restricted column set.

Fixed data and customer demands

The problem is defined on the complete directed graph

G = (V,A),V = {0,…,n},V ′ = {1,…,n},A = {(i,j) : i,j ∈ V,i≠j}.

Node 0 is the depot and V ′ is the customer set. The fixed cost of traversing arc (i,j) ∈ A is cij. Vehicles form a homogeneous fleet, with capacity Q per vehicle. The fixed target fill rate is F.

Section 3 assumes that customer demands are independent and identically distributed. For each customer, demand is a discrete random variable satisfying

ξi ∈ ℕ,𝔼[ξi] = μi,ℙ(ξi = 0) = 0,i ∈ V ′.

Demand is revealed only upon arrival at the customer’s location. Allocation to the current customer is decided without knowing the demands of customers still to be visited. A customer’s fill rate is the proportion of its demand supplied.

Full feasible-route set and route coefficients

A route has the form

r = (i0,i1,…,im,im+1),i0 = im+1 = 0,ik ∈ V ′(k ∈{1,…,m}).

The set R contains exactly those routes that start and end at the depot, visit each customer at most once, and satisfy the collective-fill-rate requirement

r ≥ F. 		(3)

Here r is the route’s collective fill rate, defined by (1)–(2) and the evaluation relation below. Requirement (3) applies to each route admitted to R; it is not an additional restriction on the selection variables outside this definition.
