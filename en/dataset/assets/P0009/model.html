Retained Core Model

According to the tex formulas and the PDF, the retained core mathematical model is the original hurricane disaster relief multi-stage stochastic mixed-integer linear program (HDR) on Pages 22–23. The Page 4 generic MSILP, the Page 6 partially extended formulation, and the later aggregated/decision-rule/decomposition formulations are omitted because they are reformulations or solution methods built on top of the original core problem.

Decision variables. At each scenario-tree node n, xnjI is the inventory at DC j, xnjc is the capacity at DC j, vj is production at DC j, yij is the shipped relief quantity from DC j to shelter i, wi is unmet demand at shelter i, and znℓ ∈{0,1} indicates activation of contingency modality ℓ.

Sets and parameters. I is the shelter set, J the distribution-center set, and L the set of contingency modalities. Costs are given by gj, qnj, fnij, and bi. dni is demand at shelter i. Capacity additions are Kjℓ. The scenario tree transition probabilities are p¯nn′.
