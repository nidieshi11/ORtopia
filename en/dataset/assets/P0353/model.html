P353: deterministic multimode railway freight model

This is the complete currently executed model M0 for one supplied demand scenario, with explicit OD and deadline eligibility repairs. It is a mixed integer quadratically constrained problem: products αη and δ𝜃 are retained exactly and handled by global nonconvex optimization. Each demand scenario has its own decisions and is solved independently.

Sets and fixed parameters. Y is the OD-pair set, R the freight-class set and K the train set. Ky1,Ky2,Ky3 are the compatible piggyback, reserved-coach and dedicated-freight trains for y. Each current train belongs to its specified OD. O,D,N are separate origin-role, destination-role and intermediate station sets; I = O ∪ D ∪ N. Different roles at one physical station have explicit distance/time mappings in the input. All p,q,a,u1,u2,u3,u4,w,l,c1,c2,c3,c4,c5,c6,d1,d2,t1,t2,t3,t4,t5,t6,t7,M are supplied numerical parameters, with exactly the indices in the following formulas. In particular c1,c6,t5 are scalars, ayi is OD endpoint incidence, lkr is a fixed feasibility flag for a fixed train’s scheduled arrival, t1r is freight availability and t7r its deadline. The source-style positive domains are deliberate: βk ∈{1,2,3} and ι ∈ ℤ≥1, with the stated positive-integer domains. Dedicated-train path variables obey the stated rows for both used and unused paths.
