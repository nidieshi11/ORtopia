P7: Complete path representation of indivisible fixed-cost network flows (Path-FCNF)

Source and scope According to sections 3, objectives of paths, and equations (7)–(10) on pages 1, 3, 4, and 5 of the original paper PDF file, it refers to the binary path domain.

Collections and parameters

Let D = (N,A) be the directed network and K be the set of commodities. Commodity k has an origin s(k), a destination t(k), and demand dk > 0. The unit transportation cost, activation fee, and capacity for the arc (i,j) are respectively cij ≥ 0,fij ≥ 0,uij ≥ 0. P(k) is the set of all feasible origin-to-destination paths for commodity k. A(p) is the set of arcs used by path p. In the unsplittable-demand case considered here, all demand of each commodity is routed on a single path.
