Complete physical definition

Nc represents all customers, Nd ⊂ Nc represents customers with available drone services, No = {0}∪ Nc,Ne = Nc ∪{n + 1} and A = {(i,j) : i ∈ No,j ∈ Ne,i≠j} are consistent with Table 2 on page 6 of the original PDF. The set of all nodes is N = {0}∪ Nc ∪{n + 1}. The sum of all arcs automatically excludes self-cycles and arcs that do not belong to A; explicit arc subscripts that are not summed in the constraints are also taken as A. (14d)–(14e) requires (i,j),(i′,j) ∈ A. x0,x1 represents arcs where trucks are equipped with/don’t have drones, and x2 represents drone flight arcs. The actual flight time for each k and arc is tijk = eijk∕P; unselected arcs are zero.

M is a common finite positive value given to instances; rij takes the Manhattan distance, and rij′ takes the Euclidean distance. The service duration is calculated by adding the durations for delivering and picking up goods separately.
