\section*{MILP1 for Fixed OR and PACU Capacities}

The solver implements the paper's original MILP1 from Section 4.2.1 for a
single weekday. The operating-room and PACU capacities $R_1$ and $R_2$ are
fixed input parameters, exactly as in the paper. 

Let $\mathcal{S} = \{1,2\}$, where stage $1$ is OR and stage $2$ is PACU. For
stage $s \in \mathcal{S}$, let $\mathcal{R}_s = \{1,\ldots,R_s\}$ be the set of
rooms, and let the time horizon be discretized as $\{0,\ldots,K\}$.

\begin{equation}
\min \; \Pi =
\sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_s} C_s^o h_{sr}
+
\sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} C_s^e R_s
\tag{1}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_s} \sum_{t=0}^{K-p_{is}} x_{isr}^t = 1,
\qquad
\forall i \in \mathcal{I},\ \forall s \in \mathcal{S}
\tag{2}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} \sum_{\tau=\max\{0,t-p_{is}\}}^{t-1} x_{isr}^{\tau} \le 1,
\qquad
\forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall r \in \mathcal{R}_s,\ \forall t = 1,\ldots,K
\tag{3}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{\tau=t}^{\min\{K-1,t+p_{is}-1\}} y_{isr}^{\tau} \ge p_{is} x_{isr}^t,
\qquad
\forall i \in \mathcal{I},\ \forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall r \in \mathcal{R}_s,\ \forall t = 0,\ldots,K-p_{is}
\tag{4}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_s} y_{isr}^t = z_s^t,
\qquad
\forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall t = 0,\ldots,K-1
\tag{5}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{t=0}^{K-1} z_s^t = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} p_{is},
\qquad
\forall s \in \mathcal{S}
\tag{6}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_s} \sum_{t=0}^{K-p_{is}} (t+p_{is}) x_{isr}^t = f_{is},
\qquad
\forall i \in \mathcal{I},\ \forall s \in \mathcal{S}
\tag{7}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_2} \sum_{t=0}^{K-p_{i2}} t \, x_{i2r}^t \ge f_{i1},
\qquad
\forall i \in \mathcal{I}
\tag{8}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
f_{i2} - (f_{i1} + p_{i2}) \le 0,
\qquad
\forall i \in \mathcal{I}
\tag{9}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
(t+1-g_{sr}) \le K\omega_{sr}^t,
\qquad
\forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall r \in \mathcal{R}_s,\ \forall t = 0,\ldots,K-1
\tag{10}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} y_{isr}^t \le K(1-\omega_{sr}^t),
\qquad
\forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall r \in \mathcal{R}_s,\ \forall t = 0,\ldots,K-1
\tag{11}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
g_{sr} - T \le h_{sr},
\qquad
\forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall r \in \mathcal{R}_s
\tag{12}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
z_s^t \le R_s,
\qquad
\forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall t = 0,\ldots,K-1
\tag{13}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
x_{isr}^t,\ y_{isr}^t,\ \omega_{sr}^t \in \{0,1\},
\qquad
\forall i \in \mathcal{I},\ \forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall r \in \mathcal{R}_s,\ \forall t = 0,\ldots,K-1
\tag{14}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
z_s^t,\ f_{is},\ g_{sr},\ h_{sr} \ge 0,
\qquad
\forall i \in \mathcal{I},\ \forall s \in \mathcal{S},\ \forall r \in \mathcal{R}_s,\ \forall t = 0,\ldots,K-1
\tag{15}
\end{equation}

Constraints (2)--(7) assign each patient and prevent overlap, including
the first occupied slot. Equation (3) uses the original paper's window
$[\max(0,t-p_{is}),t-1]$ at $t=1,\ldots,K$ (PDF page 15).
Completion variable $f_{is}$ equals actual completion; this makes (8)--(9)
enforce the no-wait transfer described in the source (PDF pages 14--15).


\paragraph{Start-time domain clarification (2026-10-23).}
Each $p_{is}$ is a positive integer number of slots. Define $x_{isr}^t=0$
for $K-p_{is}<t\le K-1$; only $0\le t\le K-p_{is}$ are start decisions.
Every sum that formally contains a later start uses this zero convention.
The original PDF page 15 imposes its service-covering inequality (4) at
all start times: for a late start fewer than $p_{is}$ binary occupancy
slots remain, so that inequality already forces the start to zero.
This explicit convention restores that boundary after restricting (4) to
service-completable times, and matches the current solver's start-variable range.
It removes only redundant late records from the previously extracted model;
setting those records to zero preserves every actual schedule and its objective.
