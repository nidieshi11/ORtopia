Integrated Fleet Assignment, Cruise Speed, and Aircraft Swaps


1 Sets and parameters

Let L, F, and P be finite sets of flights, aircraft types, and original flight paths, respectively. Let Ld ⊆ L be the flights with prescribed delays, S(l) ⊆ L the possible swap partners of flight l, and L(p) ⊆ L the flights on path p. Define

E = {(l,j) : l ∈ L,j ∈ S(l)}.

All referenced indices must be covered by the instance: in particular, (j,l) ∈ E whenever (l,j) ∈ E. The mappings n(l) ∈ L, fl ∈ F, and lo(p) ∈ L(p) specify the immediate successor of flight l, its original aircraft type, and the first flight of path p considered in rescheduling.

All parameters below are scalar arrays over the indicated indices:

  • For l ∈ L: scheduled departure dl, scheduled arrival rl, turnaround time al, cruise distance dlcr, departure-delay bound ℓlu, arrival-delay cost coefficient wl, passenger count psgl, and coefficient spl for sl.
  • For l ∈ Ld: prescribed delay ℓ¯l.
  • For (l,f) ∈ L × F: noncruise time tlfnc, original speed vlfo, upper speed bound vlfu, and fuel coefficients ci,lf for i = 1,2,3,4. These coefficients denote the applicable coefficients of the supplied fuel function; shared coefficients are represented by equal entries.
  • For (l,j) ∈ E: swap cost hlj. For f ∈ F: seat capacity bf.
  • Fuel price pfuel, carbon price pCO2, fuel-to-emissions conversion factor κ, and incremental emissions limit Cmax ⁡ .

The original assigned speeds have defined positive-speed baseline fuel evaluations. Define the constant

B0 = ∑ l∈Ldlcr (c 1,lfl(vlflo)2 + c 2,lflvlflo + c3,lfl (vlflo)2 + c4,lfl (vlflo)3 ) .

2 Variables and domains

For every (l,f) ∈ L × F, zlf denotes aircraft-type assignment, ℓlf departure delay, vlf cruise speed, and tlf cruise time. For (l,j) ∈ E, xlj denotes a swap. For l ∈ L, sl is the passenger-related variable in the stated capacity inequality. The scalar ℓl for l ∈ Ld is distinct from the fleet-indexed variables ℓlf and is governed by its stated constraints.

The variables Al,Dl represent the arrival-delay argument and its nonnegative part. The variables qlf,Ulf,Rlf,Hlf,Blf give the exact fuel graph. Their domains, together with those of the original variables, are

zlf ∈{0,1}, l ∈ L,f ∈ F, (1) xlj ∈{0,1}, (l,j) ∈ E, (2) ℓlf,vlf,tlf,qlf,Ulf,Rlf,Hlf ≥ 0, l ∈ L,f ∈ F, (3) Blf ∈ ℝ, l ∈ L,f ∈ F, (4) sl,Dl ≥ 0,Al ∈ ℝ, l ∈ L, (5) ℓl ∈ ℝ, l ∈ Ld. (6)

All variables except x and z are continuous.
