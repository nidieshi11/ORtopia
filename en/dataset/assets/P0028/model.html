Sets and input

A is the entire supplied transport-arc set. For each a, its positive integer frequency grid Θa, monthly capacity per departure Ja, fixed cost Fa and transport mode are supplied. T = 720 hours; all times and deadlines use hours. Every shipment k ∈ K belongs to exactly one shipper class with time value vk and reliability value rk, and demand qk > 0. Lk contains all simple supplied-network paths, with transfers included in path time tkl and variable cost ckl. ukl is the sum of unreliable arc travel times. Dk is the delivery deadline. Uk > 0 is the outside-option generalized cost. KC ⊆ K has a competing service with generalized cost Ck > 0. W is the carrier’s waiting cost and Ψ the unused-capacity penalty. Prices and outside costs have consistent currency units.
