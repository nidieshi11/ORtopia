Scope and sources. The selected model is the paper’s full formulation, not its fixed-base-case contingency subproblem or an active-set subproblem. The defining families below follow the paper, physical pp. 3–6, and its reference to the official SCOPF Problem Formulation: Challenge 1, updated April 9, 2019 (82 PDF pages). The single-component-outage version specified by that official document is used for the detailed contingency sets. The paper also discusses datasets containing multiple-component outages; those are not imposed on this selected single-outage version. The paper’s squared rating construction and complementarity disjunctions are retained. The official document is not treated as a corrected edition of the paper.

Variable roles and abstract functions. The continuous base-case controls u0 are generator active and reactive powers and controllable shunt susceptances. The only contingency controls uk are controllable shunt susceptances. All other variables are states, except the penalized slacks. Thus pg,qg,biCS are base-case controls; bikCS are contingency controls; voltage magnitudes, voltage angles, branch flows, contingency generator powers, and the contingency response scale Δk are state variables. Cost interpolation coefficients and cost variables are auxiliary variables. No binary response or discrete shunt variables are introduced.

The functions a0,ak capture the flow definitions below; a0 also fixes one base-case phase angle to zero. The functions b0,bk capture the bus balances, c0,ck the rating families, and τkL,τkR the response complementarity families. The paper does not give explicit vector assembly formulas for these functions. Its bounds remain written as contingency-dependent in (2.8). The detailed bounds below are the referenced competition version. The model uses a balanced single-phase equivalent representation, fixed transformer taps and phase shifts, and continuously variable controllable shunts.
