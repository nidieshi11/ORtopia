All inactive subsets are enumerated for the original six-site instance. On the large tier the same cuts on integer empty-flight counts are implemented by a single-commodity connectivity flow. Write K for the number of delivery components without an initially occupied locker. Send K units from the occupied locker to one representative of each of these components. On a directed arc ij the flow is bounded by K(xij + xji + aij), where aij = 1 if either direction is a required delivery arc. Every operationally feasible solution satisfies these degree and connection conditions, so the graph optimum is also a lower bound on the operational empty-flight distance.

Full operational feasibility and certificate. An operational solution must execute every labelled task once, together with xij empty flights of each OD. Each flight is assigned to a drone at its origin; the drone may not overlap another flight, departures may not precede release/loading readiness, and the number of parked drones at any site may never exceed cv or fall below zero.
