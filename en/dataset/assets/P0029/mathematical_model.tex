\section*{Chance-Constrained Multiple Bin Packing}

Assume the random item sizes admit an explicitly enumerated finite support
$\Omega$ with realization $\xi_i^\omega$ and probability $p_\omega$ for each
$\omega \in \Omega$. Then the chance constraint admits the following exact
mixed-integer reformulation, which is the solver-ready form used by Gurobi.

\begin{equation}
\text{(CBP-IP)} \qquad \min_{\boldsymbol{x}, \boldsymbol{y}, \boldsymbol{z}}
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{J}} c_j^a x_j
+ \sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{J}} c_{ij}^b y_{ij}
\tag{1a}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
y_{ij} \le x_j,
\qquad \forall i \in \mathcal{I},\ j \in \mathcal{J}
\tag{1b}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{J}} y_{ij} = 1,
\qquad \forall i \in \mathcal{I}
\tag{1c}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} \xi_i^\omega y_{ij}
+ \left(M_j^\omega - t_j\right) z_j^\omega
\le M_j^\omega,
\qquad \forall j \in \mathcal{J},\ \omega \in \Omega
\tag{1d}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\sum_{\omega \in \Omega} p_\omega z_j^\omega \ge 1 - \varepsilon,
\qquad \forall j \in \mathcal{J}
\tag{1e}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
x_j \in \{0,1\}, \quad y_{ij} \in \{0,1\}, \quad z_j^\omega \in \{0,1\},
\qquad \forall i \in \mathcal{I},\ j \in \mathcal{J},\ \omega \in \Omega
\tag{1f}
\end{equation}

Here, $z_j^\omega = 1$ means that bin $j$ satisfies its capacity constraint in
scenario $\omega$, and $M_j^\omega$ is any valid big-$M$ coefficient. A safe
choice is $M_j^\omega \ge \sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} \xi_i^\omega$; tighter
strengthened values may be substituted when available.
