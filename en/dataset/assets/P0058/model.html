Cloud Server Deployment Under Demand Uncertainty

Source: Section 3.2, PDF pp.10–14, (1)–(18), and Section 5.2, PDF p.23. This is one finite-scenario, CVaR version of the complete two-stage cloud server deployment model. It retains both stages as one coupled problem.

Sets and data. L denotes data centers, S suppliers, D1 known cluster requests, and Dω2 the newly revealed requests in scenario ω ∈Ω. The scenarios are equally weighted, as in the source’s sampled formulation and the retained input. T = {0,…,T}, T1 = {0,…,T1} and T2 = {T1 + 1,…,T}. A row ordered now becomes available only in T2. L(d),S(d) and their scenario-indexed versions are compatible data centers and suppliers. H contains pairs (p,t), where p ⊆L is a possibly overlapping hierarchical throughput group. cd,t,ud,hd,s are nonnegative early/late docking, unfulfilled-demand and shipping costs; superscript ω denotes the scenario data. bℓ is the row-building cost, ζℓ total row capacity, and ρℓ,1,σs,1,δp,t,1 are initial free rows, supplier inventory and throughput. The capacity data are nonnegative integers. Incompatible variables are absent and count as zero in sums.

Variables. xℓ is the number of new rows. Binary zd,ℓ,t docks a cluster request and wd,s selects its supplier. The second-stage counterparts carry superscript ω. Residual resources ρ2,σ2,δ2 are shared by all scenarios and cannot anticipate their realizations. qω is the full nonnegative scenario cost. η ∈ ℝ, eω ≥ 0, and 𝜃 ∈ ℝ define CVaR at confidence level α ∈ [0,1). The paper’s tail-mass notation is 1 − α.
