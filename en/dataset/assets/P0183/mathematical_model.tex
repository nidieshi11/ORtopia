\section*{P183: complete inbound logistics model with consistent shipment units}
This independently repaired version retains the substantive mode, inventory,
delivery-pattern and capacity families of the paper. It corrects the extracted
indices and the overloaded price/weight notation, using the explicit indices and meanings below.

\subsection*{Input and variables}
Suppliers are $i\in L$, dates $j\in D=\{1,\ldots,N\}$ with positive even $N$,
modes $M=\{\mathrm{FTL},\mathrm{CES},\mathrm{LTL}\}$, frequencies $o\in O$,
zones $z\in Z$, storage resources $h\in H$, LTL weight classes $b\in Q$
and CES classes $k\in K$. $d_i>0$ is horizon shipment volume; $q$ and $s$
are fractions of it. $SS_i$ is a safety-stock fraction. $f_i$ is freight weight
per volume, $e_i$ the empty-container volume factor, and $\omega_i$ its weight
per empty volume. All tariff coefficients $c^L_{ib},c^C_k$ are separate from
weight-class bounds $(L^L_{ib},U^L_{ib})$ and $(L^C_k,U^C_k)$.
The zero CES-weight class has a zero tariff. The implementation's JSON names
are respectively fwq, fSLC, omega\_ec, Bp, BpCES, WeightLo/WeightHi and CESLo/CESHi.

Binary variables are $v_i^m,p_{ij}^m,\gamma_i,\tau_{ij},\beta_{io},
\alpha_{bij},\alpha^{ec}_{bij},\delta_{kij}$.
$0\le q_{ij}^m\le1$, $s_{ij}\ge0$ including $s_{i0}$, and
$w_{bij},w^{ec}_{bij},w^C_{kij}\ge0$.
$n_{ij},n^{ec}_{ij},n^L_{jz}$ are nonnegative integers.
All sums below span the declared index sets; the constraints indexed by half
period use $j=1,\ldots,N/2$.

\subsection*{Objective}
\begin{align}
\min\quad&
\sum_{i,j}C_i^D(n_{ij}+n^{ec}_{ij})
+\sum_{i,j,k}c_k^C\delta_{kij}
+\sum_{i,j,b}c^L_{ib}(w_{bij}+w^{ec}_{bij})\\
&+A\sum_{i,j,m}p_{ij}^m
+N\sum_{i,o}(C_i^{dR}u_{io}^R+C_i^{dI}u_{io}^I)\beta_{io}.
\end{align}
The five cost components are added, as in the paper, page 6, equation (1).
\subsection*{Demand, inventory and mode choice}
\begin{align}
\sum_{j,m}q_{ij}^m&=1,&
s_{i0}+\sum_{\ell=1}^j\sum_mq_{i\ell}^m&\ge j/N,\\
s_{ij}&=s_{i,j-1}+\sum_mq_{ij}^m-1/N+SS_i\mathbf1_{\{j=1\}},\\
\sum_m v_i^m&=1,&
\sum_m p_{ij}^m&\le1,&
q_{ij}^m&\le p_{ij}^m\le v_i^m,\\
q_{ij}^m&\ge(Q^{min}/d_i)p_{ij}^m\quad(m=\mathrm{FTL},\mathrm{CES}),&
q_{ij}^{LTL}&\ge p_{ij}^{LTL}/N.
\end{align}
The initial-stock fraction remains a decision variable, as in the selected
model.

\subsection*{Cyclic patterns and frequency}
\begin{align}
-\gamma_i&\le p_{ij}^m-p_{i,j+N/2}^m\le\gamma_i,\\
\sum_{j,m}p_{ij}^m&\le1+N(1-\gamma_i),\\
\sum_{j=1}^{N/2}q_{ij}^{LTL}&\le
 \sum_{j=1}^{N/2}q_{i,j+N/2}^{LTL}+\gamma_i,\\
\sum_mq_{ij}^m&\ge\tau_{ij}/N,&
\sum_j\tau_{ij}&=\sum_o o\beta_{io},&
\sum_o\beta_{io}&=1,\\
\sum_{j=1}^{N/2}\sum_m p_{ij}^m&\ge1-\gamma_i,&
\sum_{j=N/2+1}^{N}\sum_m p_{ij}^m&\ge1-\gamma_i,&
\sum_jp_{ij}^m&\ge v_i^m.
\end{align}
These inequalities define the one-way relations between $\tau$ and the delivery indicators.

\subsection*{Entrance, storage and vehicle capacities}
\begin{align}
\sum_i n_{ij}+\sum_z n^L_{jz}&\le Cap_{GI},&
\sum_i p_{ij}^{CES}&\le Cap_{WK},\\
\sum_i fqp_{hi}d_i s_{ij}&\le Cap_h,&
d_iq_{ij}^{FTL}&\le Cap_L n_{ij},\\
f_i d_iq_{ij}^{FTL}&\le Cap_{WL}n_{ij},&
e_id_iq_{ij}^{FTL}&\le Cap_Ln^{ec}_{ij},\\
e_i\omega_i d_iq_{ij}^{FTL}&\le Cap_{WL}n^{ec}_{ij},&
f_id_iq_{ij}^{CES}&\le Cap_K,\\
f_id_iq_{ij}^{LTL}&\ge\omega^{LTL}p_{ij}^{LTL},\\
\sum_i r_{iz}d_iq_{ij}^{LTL}&\le Cap_Ln^L_{jz},&
\sum_i r_{iz}f_id_iq_{ij}^{LTL}&\le Cap_{WL}n^L_{jz}.
\end{align}
The FTL empty-container weight bound now includes $d_i$, just like the LTL
empty-container weight. This is the only second-round solver-code correction.

\subsection*{Freight and empty-return weight classes}
\begin{align}
\sum_b\alpha_{bij}&=1,&
L^L_{ib}\alpha_{bij}&\le w_{bij}\le U^L_{ib}\alpha_{bij},&
\sum_bw_{bij}&=f_id_iq_{ij}^{LTL},\\
\sum_b\alpha^{ec}_{bij}&=1,&
L^L_{ib}\alpha^{ec}_{bij}&\le w^{ec}_{bij}\le U^L_{ib}\alpha^{ec}_{bij},&
\sum_bw^{ec}_{bij}&=e_i\omega_i d_iq_{ij}^{LTL},\\
\sum_k\delta_{kij}&=1,&
L^C_k\delta_{kij}&\le w^C_{kij}\le U^C_k\delta_{kij},&
\sum_kw^C_{kij}&=f_i d_iq_{ij}^{CES}.
\end{align}

\subsection*{Source and repair evidence}
Paper pp.6--8 and15--16, equations (6),(17),(18),(38)--(49), distinguish the
fractional shipment quantity and physical capacity units. The source-linked
first repair separates price rates from weight bounds, restores the missing
$b$ index on $w$, and restores $Q^{min}/d_i$, $SS_i$ and storage factor $d_i$.
The second repair corrects the remaining FTL empty-weight factor.
For all ten current instances, $\omega_i Cap_L\le Cap_{WL}$ and $d_i\ge1$,
so both the former weak weight inequality and the corrected inequality are
implied by empty-volume capacity. This explains preservation of the previous
values on these inputs; the corrected code is nevertheless rerun for all ten.
