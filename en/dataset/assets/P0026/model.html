2 Definitions completing the retained QC model (2026-10-23)

The source PDF pages 6, 8–10 and 12 provide the following definitions. Each physical line appears once in A; AR only supplies its reverse flow representation. The subset NL consists of zero-load leaf buses, each with exactly one incident physical line, denoted a(i). The set-indexed switching symbol in (11a) is za(i). Complex generation bounds apply componentwise, and

Skg = p kg + iq kg,S ij = pij + iqij,Wij = wijR + iw ijI.

Transformer real/imaginary components are tijR,tijI; charging-admittance components are gijc,bijc. The star denotes complex conjugation. Positive voltage lower bounds and nonzero transformer ratios are required. This retained expression uses nondegenerate angle intervals strictly inside (−π∕2,π∕2). Writing a = 𝜃̲ij < b = 𝜃¯ij for each line,

𝜃iju = max ⁡ (|a|,|b|) > 0,𝜃 ijc = cos ⁡ b − cos ⁡ a b − a ,𝜃ijs = sin ⁡ b − sin ⁡ a b − a .

The sine bounds are sin ⁡ a,sin ⁡ b and the cosine lower bound is min ⁡ (cos ⁡ a,cos ⁡ b); the cosine upper bound is 1 when a ≤ 0 ≤ b and otherwise max ⁡ (cos ⁡ a,cos ⁡ b). The input 𝜃M is a given valid finite off-state angle allowance, as in the retained inequalities. For each line, the eight ξ points are the Cartesian product of the two endpoint voltage-bound pairs and the cosine-bound pair; the eight γ points use the sine-bound pair instead. The omitted line subscripts on ξ,γ are implicit. Both lists use first voltage slowest and the trigonometric coordinate fastest: lower/lower/lower, lower/lower/upper, lower/upper/lower, lower/upper/upper, upper/lower/lower, upper/lower/upper, upper/upper/lower, upper/upper/upper. Finally,

viσ = v̲ i + v¯i,ϕij = a + b 2 ,δij = b − a 2 ,l¯ij = |Tij|2(s¯ija)2 v̲i2 .

The instances supply these values for the line parameters.
