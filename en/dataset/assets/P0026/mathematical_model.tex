\section{Definitions completing the retained QC model (2026-10-23)}
The source PDF pages 6, 8--10 and 12 provide the following definitions.
Each physical line appears once in $\mathcal A$; $\mathcal A^R$ only supplies
its reverse flow representation. The subset $\mathcal N^L$ consists of
zero-load leaf buses, each with exactly one incident physical line, denoted
$a(i)$. The set-indexed switching symbol in (11a) is $z_{a(i)}$.
Complex generation bounds apply componentwise, and
\[
 S_k^g=p_k^g+\mathrm{i}q_k^g,\qquad
 S_{ij}=p_{ij}+\mathrm{i}q_{ij},\qquad
 W_{ij}=w_{ij}^R+\mathrm{i}w_{ij}^I.
\]
Transformer real/imaginary components are $\boldsymbol t_{ij}^R,\boldsymbol t_{ij}^I$;
charging-admittance components are $\boldsymbol g_{ij}^c,\boldsymbol b_{ij}^c$.
The star denotes complex conjugation. Positive voltage lower bounds and
nonzero transformer ratios are required. This retained expression uses
nondegenerate angle intervals strictly inside $(-\pi/2,\pi/2)$.
Writing $a=\underline\theta_{ij}<b=\overline\theta_{ij}$ for each line,
\[
 \theta_{ij}^u=\max(|a|,|b|)>0,\qquad
 \theta_{ij}^c=\frac{\cos b-\cos a}{b-a},\qquad
 \theta_{ij}^s=\frac{\sin b-\sin a}{b-a}.
\]
The sine bounds are $\sin a,\sin b$ and the cosine lower bound is
$\min(\cos a,\cos b)$; the cosine upper bound is 1 when $a\le0\le b$
and otherwise $\max(\cos a,\cos b)$. The input $\theta^M$ is a given
valid finite off-state angle allowance, as in the retained inequalities.
For each line, the eight $\xi$ points are the Cartesian product of the two
endpoint voltage-bound pairs and the cosine-bound pair; the eight $\gamma$
points use the sine-bound pair instead. The omitted line subscripts on
$\xi,\gamma$ are implicit. Both lists use first voltage slowest and the
trigonometric coordinate fastest: lower/lower/lower, lower/lower/upper,
lower/upper/lower, lower/upper/upper, upper/lower/lower,
upper/lower/upper, upper/upper/lower, upper/upper/upper.
Finally,
\[
 \boldsymbol v_i^\sigma=\underline{\boldsymbol v}_i+\overline{\boldsymbol v}_i,
 \quad\boldsymbol\phi_{ij}=\frac{a+b}{2},
 \quad\boldsymbol\delta_{ij}=\frac{b-a}{2},
 \quad\overline l_{ij}=\frac{|\boldsymbol T_{ij}|^2(\overline{\boldsymbol s}_{ij}^a)^2}
 {\underline{\boldsymbol v}_i^2}.
\]
The instances supply these values for the line parameters.
