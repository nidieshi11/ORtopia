Data premises and scope

A sequence is persistently exciting of order l when Hl of that sequence has full row rank. Assumption 2 requires, for every i ∈ ℕ1n, that uid be persistently exciting of order

Tini + N + 2 + 2mi.

The corresponding printed lower bound on local data length is

Ti ≥ 2(Tini + N + 2mi + 2) − 1,i ∈ ℕ1n. 		(34)

This length bound does not replace the full-row-rank condition.

The underlying exact linear trajectory representation in Corollary 1 additionally assumes controllability and observability. Its uniqueness statement is conditional: if Tini ≥ 2 + 2mi, the future yi is uniquely determined for every (ui,ini,𝜖i,ini,yi,ini,ui,𝜖i) by that exact representation. This is not an additional constraint on the decisions of (50). The selected regularized formulation is expressly intended for nonlinear, non-deterministic behavior and noise-corrupted data. The equal-data-length and centralized/local data-consistency hypotheses of Theorem 1 are not premises of (50).
