Hydraulic Flow–Pressure Optimization Core


1 Sets and supplied data

Let V be a finite node set, partitioned into entry nodes V +, exit nodes V −, and internal nodes V 0. Write S := V + ∪ V −. Let A be a finite directed arc set with specified tail and head for each arc, denoted a = (u,v). Define

δout(u) := {a ∈ A : tail ⁡ (a) = u},δin(u) := {a ∈ A : head ⁡ (a) = u}.

The supplied subsets Api ⊆ A and Ars-nl ⊆ A index pipes and nonlinear resistors, respectively. Set

B := Api ∪ Ars-nl.

The numerical data consist of pipe coefficients c1,a,c2,a ∈ ℝ for a ∈ Api, resistor coefficients ca ∈ ℝ for a ∈ Ars-nl, and the supplied lower and upper bounds

(pu−,p u+)(u ∈ V ),(q u−,q u+)(u ∈ S),(q a−,q a+)(a ∈ A).

The five scalar objective components f1,…,f5 are supplied finite-dimensional Gurobi-compatible expressions for the selected core. Their expression definitions and numerical coefficients are supplied by the instance. With

z :=((pu)u∈V ,(qu)u∈S,(qa)a∈A) ∈ ℝ|V |+|S|+|A|,

write these expressions as fi(z).

2 Decision variables

The continuous physical variables are pressure pu ∈ ℝ for u ∈ V , nodal supply or demand qu ∈ ℝ for u ∈ S, and arc flow qa ∈ ℝ for a ∈ A. Arc flow is positive along the specified arc direction; nodal supply is positive and demand is negative under the balance convention below.

Introduce continuous auxiliary variables

ta ∈ ℝ≥0,ra ∈ ℝ(a ∈ B),
da ∈ ℝ,wa ∈ ℝ≥0,ha ∈ ℝ(a ∈ Ars-nl).

Here ABS ⁡ (x) denotes the exact absolute-value general constraint: an equality y = ABS ⁡ (x) requires y = |x|.

Exact solver representation and selection. This is the selected hydraulic core, consisting only of the stated objective, nodal balances, pipe and nonlinear-resistor physics, and pressure and flow bounds. Equations (3) and (7) are exact absolute-value general constraints. Equations (4), (5), (8), and (9) are nonconvex quadratic equalities; Gurobi must be configured to accept nonconvex quadratic constraints.
