Scenario-distribution fitting model

Let M and D be finite index sets, and let Jm be a finite nonempty index set for each m ∈M. Let q be a positive integer. For each indexed observation i, let vi ∈ ℝq be given data.

The continuous decision variables are representative vectors v¯d ∈ ℝq for d ∈D, assignment weights Wd,i,m ∈ ℝ for m ∈M, i ∈ Jm, d ∈D, probabilities Pd,m ∈ ℝ for d ∈D, m ∈M, and an auxiliary scalar t ∈ ℝ.
