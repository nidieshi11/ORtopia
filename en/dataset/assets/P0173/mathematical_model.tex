\section*{Collection, domain, and public transportation connectivity}

\[
s_i+\sum_jx_{ji}=1,\quad f_i+\sum_jx_{ij}=1,\quad

\]

\section*{Charging at terminals and in garages}

Every station per minute $\sum\ell\le\sum_kn_{nk}$ and $\sum p\le\sum_kn_{nk}P_k$. This constraint represents the total power capacity of the charging terminals.
\[
x_{ij}=1\Rightarrow

\section*{PV, energy storage, and the power grid}
$D_{bt}$ represents the sum of the charging capacities for all terminals/garages mentioned above. Three equally likely weather scenarios are considered: $w$, which involves sharing all vehicles/charging decisions among them. Site attributes include $R_b$, $0\le P_b^{PV}\le\overline P^{PV}R_b$, and $0\le E_b^{BES}\le\overline E^{BES}R_b$. \[
v_{bwt}\le\rho_{wt}P_b^{PV},\quad v=p+c,\quad p+d+g\ge D_{bt},\quad0\le g\le\bar g_b,

\[
e_{bwt}=e_{bw,t-1}+\Delta\eta_c c_{bwt}-\Delta d_{bwt}/\eta_d,\quad
