P115: completed finite-horizon ridesharing MDP

The model is a finite-horizon tabular MDP with the transition kernel and reward table supplied by the instance.

Data and domains. There are three regions and 180 vehicles in the completed scenario. H = 12 hourly decision stages are t = 0,…,11; thus the original inclusive terminal reward index is T = H − 1 = 11. The nonterminal states are s = (t,u,v) with u,v ∈{0,1,2}, the demand and vehicle-availability regimes. Each state’s request, vacant, occupied and previous-demand quantities are explicitly stored in model_data.states. Add one absorbing terminal state †. There are 108 + 1 = 109 states. The action sets contain all regional price/wage combinations

A = {2.4,2.8,3.3}3 ×{1.3,1.65}3,|A| = 216.

The action list order is the index used by each state’s transition table. Let rsa be the table’s expected net revenue, and let Psas′ be its next-state probability. The table also records expected served counts for reporting. The reward table is the completed income-minus-wage reward, with finite availability and heterogeneous demand/trip inputs recorded in its generator. Psas′ ≥ 0 and ∑ ⁡ s′Psas′ = 1. Every nonterminal transition advances the clock by one, and all transitions from t = 11 end at †. All these entries are fixed input data. Initial distribution μ is explicitly supplied on the nine t = 0 states, μ ≥ 0, ∑ ⁡ sμs = 1; γ = 0.97.
